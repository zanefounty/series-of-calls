# Progression implementation proposal

## Status

This document is a proposed implementation and acceptance map for the 13 preserved source progression levels and 19 preserved source goals listed in [`campaign-obligations.json`](campaign-obligations.json). It is not an implementation, executable specification, or evidence that any source level or goal is complete. Their obligation statuses remain `specified-pending-implementation` and their acceptance statuses remain `not-executed`.

The current campaign engine and its tests cover campaign windows, service evidence, scenario constraints, and an assessed award sequence for each of four tracks through real shared dispatch. The optional progression module now has candidate mechanics for `progress.progression-level.1`, `progress.goal.bb`, `progress.goal.bz`, `progress.goal.ix`, `progress.goal.rd`, `progress.goal.tb`, and `progress.goal.t10`. AC-SOC-141–148 test this module's content preservation, evidence predicates, reward behavior, product binding hook, save validation, purity, and worker serialization. AC-SOC-157–160 add selected shared-dispatch, product-binding, worker, restore, and learning-control coverage. AC-SOC-190–193 add sampled-workload and transport-continuity award predicates, with independent observed source proofs and retained historical awards. The symbolic case keys below name proposed full source-row acceptance targets; they are not AC IDs. Existing cases cover selected mechanics, not every target in the tables.

## Shared implementation contract

Progression should be a separate layer over the campaign evidence already collected:

- A level unlock records a capability tier. A goal records a once-only milestone. Neither represents a regulatory approval, live external service, or automatic customer success.
- Every positive check should consume typed, validated evidence with stable IDs: effective resource versions, accepted contracts, completed journeys with retained case context, observation windows, support ownership, and/or billing periods as applicable. A submitted configuration or narrative flag alone is not evidence.
- Evidence should retain the source campaign revision, scenario/track, tenant, jurisdiction profile, and relevant resource generation. Relevant configuration changes make affected evidence stale. An old successful interaction cannot be reused as new evidence after a changed requirement.
- Keep people, licences, seats, interactions, messages, bandwidth, sites, geographic markets, integrations, and money as separate units. Never infer staffed capacity from licence count, service quality from a throughput peak, or a new region from a jurisdiction label.
- For qualifications and jurisdiction-specific scope, require an explicit scenario capability and tested configuration. Do not model legal approval or certification as an automatic consequence of reaching a numeric threshold.
- Goal rewards, if retained, need a unique achievement ledger key and should apply once. Calculate, inspect, reload, duplicate commands, sandbox snapshots, or merely satisfying readiness must not issue the reward again.
- Each acceptance case should have a positive fixture satisfying the contract and a negative fixture that removes or forges one required fact. Where persistence or awards are involved, repeat after save/restore and assert idempotence.

Source thresholds should remain provenance and balancing references until a product review defines their communications equivalents. The implementation must not convert source Gbit/s, POP, customer, seat, or cash values directly into sessions, seats, site count, or AUD.

## Current model and remaining review

The original proposal predates concurrent portfolio roots, distinct geographic market records, independent admission controls, site/failure-domain exercises and scoped product evidence. Those capabilities now exist as versioned optional packs. The calling, site, market and capability overlays provide bounded predicates across the 13 levels and 19 goals. See [calling achievements](WP-CALLING-ACHIEVEMENTS.md), [site operations](WP-SITE-OPERATIONS.md), [market qualifications](WP-MARKET-QUALIFICATION.md) and [capability progression](WP-CAPABILITY-PROGRESSION.md) for their actual evidence contracts and limits. Executed acceptance IDs are maintained in `../acceptance-plan.json`, with source links in `../implementation.json`.

The table below remains the full source-review target, rather than a claim that the bounded mechanics implement every original predicate. In particular, market IDs do not establish legal approvals; deployment modes do not establish geographic footprint; campaign awards alone do not establish complex contract acceptance; and synthetic product evidence does not substitute for accepted jurisdiction research. The original source attributes and unreviewed distinctive behaviours remain visible in the obligation register.

## Review and implementation sequence

1. Review these typed evidence contracts against the preserved source records and the four campaign tracks. Confirm which source attributes are provenance only and define any actual scenario thresholds in the relevant content pack.
2. Implement progression evaluation as a pure calculation over validated records; make achievement application an explicit command with stable, once-only ledger keys.
3. Add a focused module test for every row and shared-dispatch integration tests for unlocks, rewards, save/restore, stale evidence, and track switching. The symbolic case keys in the tables must not be added to the acceptance registry as IDs; allocate real acceptance IDs only after tests exist and pass.
4. Update obligation statuses only for source rows whose full reviewed contract is implemented and covered by passing acceptance tests. Partial mechanics must remain partial; do not bulk-mark by filename or module presence.

The runtime content distinguishes implemented candidate mechanics from full source dispositions. All 32 full source obligations remain `specified-pending-implementation` in the obligation register until their complete contracts are reviewed and verified. Bounded milestone tests do not change those source-level dispositions or establish complete progression fidelity.
