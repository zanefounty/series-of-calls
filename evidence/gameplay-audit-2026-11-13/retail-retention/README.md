# Provider retail retention price choice

The [Chromium report](retail-browser-report.json) records two desktop careers played through visible controls from the built site at the same seed, plan, paid stages, incident responses and renewal decisions. The first [reviewed A$1,800 per period](retail-retention-desktop/full-price/visible-price-and-retention-quote.png); the second [entered A$1,710 per period](retail-retention-desktop/discounted/visible-price-and-retention-quote.png) through Advanced retail terms and reviewed the 5% retention threshold and A$90 period revenue tradeoff before committing.

Both careers funded the three service stages and two incident repairs, accepted the first two renewal offers, and ran to minute 600. Their jobs and incident activation/resolution times matched. The final three full periods were unqualified, unqualified, qualified in both careers. At the [full-price outcome](retail-retention-desktop/full-price/retention-outcome.png), the customer exited and shared cash was A$14,453.25. At the [discounted outcome](retail-retention-desktop/discounted/retention-outcome.png), the customer offered renewal and shared cash was A$13,751.25. Credits still applied for failed service.

The visible [full-price export](retail-retention-desktop/full-price/career-export.json) and [discounted export](retail-retention-desktop/discounted/career-export.json) match the SHA-256 values in the report. All quote, paid service, repair, renewal and export screenshots are retained in those career directories. The report passed with `noSourceDrift: true`, Chromium 156.0.8078.4 and no browser console or page errors. This is one synthetic retention situation; uncoached human comprehension and later portfolio pricing remain open.
