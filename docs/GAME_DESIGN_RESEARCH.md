# Game-design research and Codex guidance

Reviewed **13 November 2026 (UTC)** against Series of Calls commit [`d7de3b91d29741640b7aec74acc134f866899f62`](https://github.com/zanefounty/series-of-calls/commit/d7de3b91d29741640b7aec74acc134f866899f62). This is a dated research and provenance guide, not a fresh gameplay acceptance receipt.

## Purpose and authority

The repository already has detailed domain rules and implementation checks. The missing discipline is asking whether those rules give the player understandable, consequential decisions with useful feedback and a reason to continue. More products, menus or successful automated commands do not answer that question.

This work adds two original, instruction-only repository skills: [soc-game-design](../.agents/skills/soc-game-design/SKILL.md) and [soc-playtest](../.agents/skills/soc-playtest/SKILL.md). The specialist roles `soc_game_designer` and `soc_playtester` extend the existing coordinator, builder and verifier workflow. Their responsibilities and invocation examples belong in [Agent workflow](AGENT_WORKFLOW.md); this page explains the research behind them.

The [versioned specification](../spec/README.md) remains the product authority. The [development workflow](DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md), [research intake](RESEARCH_GAMEPLAY_INTAKE.md) and existing acceptance process govern implementation and promotion. This synthesis creates no accepted product requirement, source disposition, release gate or additional approval ceremony. It does not establish that gameplay has improved. Historical reviews remain leads to reproduce against the relevant build.

## Recent practice and durable foundations

Research covered recent first-party development reports and established design research. A publication date is not evidence that a method works for this game. The UC/CC applications below are our design hypotheses; the sources did not test Series of Calls.

| Primary source and date | Useful method | Proposed application and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Factorio Friday Facts 441](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-441), 5 June 2026 | Automate delivery from player intent; give batching explicit priorities and a fallback when waiting would stall progress. | Let a customer intent drive routine provisioning while retaining cost, capacity and failure. Test partial supply and deadlock recovery. This is a particular logistics solution, not a reason to automate every decision. |
| [Factorio Friday Facts 448](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-448), 9 October 2026 | Connect risk to visible state; inspect how a formula rewards awkward optimization and retune using playtests and reported failures. | Show service pressure and recovery consequences before commitment. Probe whether a plan, timing trick or repetitive action wins for the wrong reason. Visible arithmetic alone does not establish fair difficulty. |
| [Against the Storm 1.11](https://eremitegames.com/rebel-hideout-update-1-11/), 24 September 2026 | Improve contextual alerts, explain exact bonuses and free workers when their work is done. Check combinations of modifiers. | Highlight the relevant next action near the affected customer or room; remove repeated administration. Check simultaneous sector constraints and incidents. Patch notes establish changes, not a controlled engagement result. |
| [Brian Cronin, GDC 2026](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2026/Slides/Cronin_Brian_PlaytestingProcessForUltraSmallTeams.pdf) | Run small, repeated hypothesis-to-playtest-to-action cycles while observing the whole experience. | Use short novice sessions and fix the highest-impact misunderstanding before adding content. Small qualitative rounds reveal problems; they do not estimate population retention or prove universal enjoyment. |
| [MDA, Hunicke, LeBlanc and Zubek](https://www.cs.northwestern.edu/~hunicke/MDA.pdf), 2004 | Distinguish rules, the behavior those rules produce during play, and the intended experience. | Trace a capacity rule through decisions and outcomes to the desired feeling of competent operation. A feature inventory only describes the first layer. MDA is an analysis framework, not a numeric fun formula. |
| [Agency Reconsidered, Wardrip-Fruin et al.](https://eis.ucsc.edu/papers/nwf-C7-digra09-agency.pdf), 2009 | Examine whether actions the player wants to take are supported and produce intelligible responses. | Match the promise of running a communications business to meaningful commitments, alternatives and consequences. More buttons do not automatically increase agency. |
| [Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf), 2006 | Examine autonomy, competence and relatedness in player motivation. | Offer comprehensible alternatives, feedback that helps mastery and relationships affected by service delivery. Treat these as hypotheses to investigate, not fixed player types or guaranteed retention levers. |
| [Celia Hodent, onboarding and engagement](https://celiahodent.com/gamers-brain-ux-onboarding/), GDC 2016; page updated 2020 | Design around attention, perception and memory; teach through relevant action and feedback. | Teach one opening service promise before exposing the full catalogue. Observe missed cues and mistaken predictions. Do not interpret necessary strategic complexity as a usability defect automatically. |
| [Matthew Davis, Into the Breach postmortem](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2019/presentations/Into%20the%20Breach%20Postmortem%20Final.pdf), GDC 2019 | Iterate on a focused decision space and the information needed to reason about consequences. | Give capacity and migration choices comparable forecasts, then test whether players can predict the result. A turn-based tactics example does not justify importing its entire structure into this simulation. |
| [Emily Short, narrative system responsibilities](https://emshort.blog/2022/04/09/what-does-your-narrative-system-need-to-do/), 9 April 2022 | Separate selecting content, managing available options and resolving consequences. | Make customer history change later opportunities, trust or contract options. Check story eligibility, recurrence and empty pools. Additional prose without changed state or options is not sufficient evidence of consequence. |
| [Soren Johnson, Claims](https://www.designer-notes.com/otc-designer-notes-3-claims/), 17 April 2016; [HQ types](https://www.designer-notes.com/otc-designer-notes-16-hq-types/), 30 April 2016 | Use scarcity and specialization to create tradeoffs; avoid rewards for frantic repeated rebuilding. Let strategic identity respond to relevant information. | Differentiate customer, MSP, SI and provider incentives, then expose a shared contract. Test opportunity costs and diminishing returns. These competitive-game examples inform hypotheses, not a mandate for multiplayer. |

The practical emphasis is on decision quality and execution burden. A choice can remain strategically difficult while its controls become easier to use. Conversely, removing every constraint can make a smoother interface and a poorer game. Evaluate both effects.

## Codex structure and evaluation

Current [official skill documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills), checked 13 November 2026, places repository skills in `.agents/skills`. The `SKILL.md` name and description guide selection; full instructions load when selected. `agents/openai.yaml` provides optional presentation metadata. Explicit local invocation uses `$soc-game-design` or `$soc-playtest`.

Current [custom-agent documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/subagents) uses standalone `.codex/agents/*.toml` files with `name`, `description` and `developer_instructions`. Agents inherit parent permissions. A role described as read-only does not acquire a separate sandbox through that description. Preserve existing model choices, MCP configuration and the project's concurrency limits.

The [11 September 2026 OpenAI guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra) supports narrow triggers, short routers and references loaded only when relevant. This is why the change adds focused skills instead of a catalogue of overlapping game-development packages. Routine wording or dependency fixes should not trigger a full design review.

The [4 September 2026 game-building account](https://developers.openai.com/blog/how-to-build-games-with-astra) provides a useful engineering distinction: repeatable named scenes help investigate problems, while separate journeys through real controls test reachability and transitions. Apply both when warranted. A convenient checkpoint cannot prove that a new player can reach it.

The [22 January 2026 skill-evaluation guide](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) recommends representative prompts, captured actions and artifacts, and explicit checks. Its historical path examples do not override current discovery documentation. Evaluate invocation and task behavior separately: does the right skill load, and does it produce a defensible decision or experiment without unnecessary work?

Useful evaluation cases include a service-plan comparison, a click-heavy onboarding journey, role convergence and a state-dependent customer story. Negative controls include a typo correction, dependency update and historical-summary request. Independent reviewers should receive raw artifacts and task scope rather than the intended diagnosis. Syntax checks establish parseability; an agent exercise establishes only what that run demonstrated. Neither substitutes for a human playtest.

## Applying the research without expanding the bureaucracy

For one affected feature, define the player's promise and the choice that supports it. Compare feasible alternatives from equivalent states over the same relevant horizon, including money, workload, delay, recovery and future obligations. Observe the complete UI journey separately. Preserve meaningful commitment while simplifying repeated execution. Carry the result into the existing bounded implementation contract.

For UC/CC tracks, distinguish business role, scale, sector and specialty. A government or defence constraint should change relevant decisions; it need not create another full career menu. A supplier relationship should have authority, capacity and settlement consequences before it is presented as a different play style. These are proposals to test against the accepted scope.

Keep conclusions proportional to evidence. Source inspection, deterministic simulation, browser observation, expert judgment and human participation answer different questions. A plan is not an executed test, and a completed test is not proof that the game is enjoyable. This guide records the basis for improving that process; validation results belong in their own dated records.
