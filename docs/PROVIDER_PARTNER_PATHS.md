# Paid partner connection implementation

The workplace-to-suite calling path now has Provider purchases, map controls,
native compatibility testing and actual customer-call delivery. It represents
`uc-new:family:microsoft:workplace-phone` and
`uc-new:family:cisco:suite-calling`; it does not complete either source family.

Both calling products must already be paid and commissioned. The connection
costs 98,000 cents setup and 13,000 cents per period, with ten minutes of one
engineer and one support worker. These are fictional scenario terms. The quote
retains the exact prerequisite projects, postings, product generations and paid
support scope. Installing its directional edge does not award delivery.

Select the customer or project on the map and test the connection after setup.
The native product gate must accept a subsequent customer call through the
tested generations; that call must actually resolve. Only then does the project
complete and begin its recurring exposure. Setup, testing and delivered service
remain distinct. Map rendering and selection do not dispatch or charge.

A retired product stops current service while preserving the earlier delivery
receipt. Recommissioning requires the full product setup charge. After the
product scope changes, a separately paid six-minute retest costs 42,000 cents
setup and 13,000 cents per period. An unchanged successful test does not justify
another retest. The new project must again deliver actual work; the old result
is retained. Running setup prevents withdrawing its contracted endpoint.

Provider support derives from the customer's actual funded stages and shared
payroll. Its scope cannot acquire recording or other resources merely because
another integration needs them. Historical support reconstruction recognizes
the funded edge's installation and test timeline without rewriting earlier
minutes. Imported plans and delivery receipts are checked against paid records,
native product history and actual interaction events. Worker execution uses the
same factories and validation.

The bounded contract also describes API-to-human digital handling and local
speech assistance. The API adapter has native Lab evidence covering its tested
edge, operation job, channel acceptance, delivered webhook and human resolution.
Its paid Provider purchase/control path remains pending. Local speech still
requires a causal processing-admission hook and paid support before Provider
delivery can be claimed. Neither pending offer is advertised as a purchasable
Provider operation. Complete P5 progression, the other Provider proof adapters,
the full source catalogue and owner research acceptance remain separate.

Relevant tests are `provider-partner-paid-path.test.js`,
`provider-partner-paths.test.js` and `provider-partner-support.test.js`. The paid
path test uses the real map action adapter, checks refused and forged requests,
compares worker/restore continuation, and pays for retirement recovery. Browser
execution must still be recorded for each frozen candidate; DOM controls alone
do not establish browser acceptance.
