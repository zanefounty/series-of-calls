# Capability progression overlay

## Status and boundary

This work adds `soc-capability-progression-1` as a pure, synthetic assessment overlay. It does not change the preserved source disposition for any of the nine progression levels or `progress.goal.gi`; those source obligations remain partial/pending. `calculate` and `inspect` derive readiness from current roots and recorded outcomes. They do not issue a badge, alter campaign state, or assert regulatory approval, vendor support, or real-world customer delivery.

The calculator returns a ready report only when every listed record exists and is in an effective or completed state. Reports retain IDs and the effective configuration snapshot used to explain the result. Current evidence is recalculated on every call, so a later readiness/configuration change can remove a ready result. Site and continuity evidence is read from the estate/site-operations pack through its validator and inspect result; an absent pack or absent exercise is an incomplete result.

## Runtime API

`SOC_CAPABILITY_PROGRESSION` exports `PACK`, `VERSION`, `CONTENT`, `levelIds`, `goalIds`, `implementedLevels`, `implementedGoals`, `sourceIds`, `create`, `calculate`, `inspect`, `validate`, `validateEvidence` (also `evidenceExists`), `fingerprint`, and `source`. `content/capability-progression.json.runtime` mirrors those immutable runtime IDs and version fields.

- `calculate(root, hooks)` is pure and returns `{pack, version, contentVersion, levels, goals, pendingSourceIds, pendingBoundaries}`. Product integration readiness requires a caller-supplied `integrationStatus(root, integrationId)` that observes the shared product integration path; boundary readiness requires `boundaryAssessment(root, boundaryContractId)`; site readiness may use `siteHooks`.
- A level or goal report has `{sourceId, status, implementation, evidence}`. `status` is `ready` or `incomplete`; `implementation` remains `partial`.
- `validate(root, hooks)` checks root/portfolio validity and delegates to the available portfolio, product, and site pack validators. The optional hooks are `validateRoot(root)`, `portfolioHooks`, `siteHooks`, `integrationStatus(root, integrationId)`, and `boundaryAssessment(root, boundaryContractId)`. Hooks can further restrict observed facts; they cannot make missing local records pass.
- `validateEvidence(root, sourceId, evidence, {archived, hooks})` checks exact current evidence recomputation for readiness. Archived validation binds evidence back to retained source rows such as product integrations and support contracts, business ledger receipts, site exercise/migration receipts, campaign awards, service-observation proofs, information records, and resolved interactions. For bounded interaction, business-ledger, processing-job, and information-audit rings, a missing row is accepted only when its typed ID is covered by the corresponding core eviction watermark (legacy saves use the bounded-ring ordering heuristic). A missing recent receipt or tampered proof remains unverifiable. This is structural cross-linking, not a cryptographic proof against an attacker able to rewrite the full save and every receipt together.
- `fingerprint(root)` is a read-only diagnostic fingerprint, not an award receipt or cryptographic integrity guarantee.
- `source()` emits the self-contained kernel for worker use. External pack access is lazy to avoid load-order/TDZ coupling.

All included values are synthetic scenario facts. The overlay deliberately does not expose user-authored `complete` flags or claim full source-row coverage.

## Capability evidence contracts

| Source row | Preserved name → fictional capability | Evidence required | Important failure boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 3 | Serious backbone → UC and CC integrator | A currently verified, directional product integration; at least one resolved, evidence-backed human task; and a successful room/meeting outcome in the same root. | A connector check, bot acknowledgement, or meeting configuration without resolved task evidence is incomplete. |
| Level 4 | Big league → Enterprise service provider | A completed service migration plus a completed two-site `site-failure` exercise with distinct sites, effective site/resource generations, named fault domain, successful continuity and recovery journeys, and backup/restore receipt. | A desired endpoint change, generic campaign recovery, or exercise without two distinct sites is incomplete. |
| Level 5 | Continental carrier → National communications SP | Two active, separately scoped portfolio tenants each have a supported billed contract, resolved external voice case, a carrier/wholesaler supplier role, and corresponding captured revenue and wholesale-cost receipts. | Licences, a shared carrier configuration, internal calls, or unmetered service do not qualify. |
| Level 6 | Transcontinental → Public-service communications specialist | A government campaign award, effective eligible boundary flow, completed information processing with retained in-jurisdiction record, and resolved case evidence. | A jurisdiction label, boundary contract alone, or successful call without information and task evidence is incomplete. |
| Level 8 | Packet empire → Omnichannel platform operator | One canonical person and case have separately resolved human voice and message needs under the same tenant and identity. | Separate cases, bot-only containment, delivery receipt alone, or cross-tenant identity mismatch is incomplete. |
| Level 9 | Six-continent carrier → Multi-region deployment specialist | Four distinct effective, bound, dependency-backed, tested deployments map to commercial, private, sovereign, and local profiles. | Reusing one deployment, unsupported mode strings, labels, or unprovisioned dependencies do not count as separate profiles. |
| Level 10 | Backbone of backbones → Communications wholesaler | Two active portfolio tenants have billed customer contracts through a configured distributor, nonexpired customer-granted configure authority scoped to contract resources, and revenue ledger receipts. | A partner record without customer-granted scoped authority and billed usage is incomplete. |
| Level 11 | Internet weather system → AI communications operator | A passed sustained `t10` workload proof; completed speech processing; a ready deployment generation with an actually bound and available `ai-assistance` feature; and a resolved human task using an integration. | A pending or stale deployment generation, unbound feature, unavailable trusted binding check, model response, licence, or bot-only result cannot qualify. |
| Level 12 | Too big to route → Critical-service integrator | A completed two-site `site-failure` exercise with recorded generations, bounded named fault, successful continuity/recovery journeys, backup restore receipt, and audited information read. | Unbounded outage, shared/unidentified failure domain, missing evidence custody, or recovery without a successful journey fails. |
| Goal `gi` | Sign a giant → Major enterprise or public-service contract | Four assessed campaign track awards with retained roots; enterprise and government accepted contracts with captured billing receipts, resolved call snapshots, successful meeting audit receipts, and a completed site-failure exercise and multi-site migration. | Contract size, signature, campaign title, single room join, or award without delivered retained evidence is incomplete. |

## Gaps deliberately kept visible

These predicates use evidence already represented in the runtime and therefore offer a playable synthetic progression path. They are partial implementation mechanics, not a completion of original source contracts. This overlay does not implement the distinct partner-service catalogue, external geographic-market qualification beyond portfolio market IDs, security controls as a `scrub` substitute, a full procurement/regulatory authority model, or source-native bandwidth/POP scoring. The historical source attributes and original distinctions remain in the progression obligations and content records.

Site exercise readiness depends on `soc-site-operations-1` exposing `inspect(root, hooks).exercises` with the typed exercise schema documented by that pack. If the site pack, its trusted hooks, or qualifying records are absent, levels 4 and 12 and goal `gi` remain incomplete. Level 9's deployment classes are synthetic categories inferred from exact effective product modes; they do not assert vendor certification or geographic coverage.

## Validation scope

The focused module suite has 15 passing cases, including a shared-dispatch positive for `gi`: four independently assessed campaign awards with retained track roots, active billed enterprise and government customer contracts, resolved calls, successful joined meetings, and a completed two-site fault/recovery and migration record. The progression tests also drive more than 200 additional interactions and confirm an archived voice proof remains valid only after the core eviction watermark advances; lowering that watermark invalidates the proof. The `gi` award survives cancellation and save/restore, while deleting a required track snapshot makes the still-valid save incomplete. The AI-operator predicate rejects pending deployment generations, unavailable binding checks, and deployments without a bound AI feature. Other tests cover pure calculations, stale or removed typed evidence, scope and dependency failures, product integration history, site evidence, and worker serialization. These mechanics are integrated through the parent SOC bridge, but source obligations remain partial/pending and the separate partner-service catalogue, fully modeled supplier/geographic qualification, DDoS/SIP/spend controls, and source-native bandwidth scoring remain gaps. No source-obligation disposition is changed here.
