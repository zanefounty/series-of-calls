# Commercial operations and market dynamics

The final campaign uses a consolidated ledger of the player's legal/business group. External income and costs contribute to profit once. Internal transfers between owned subsidiaries are eliminated on consolidation. A reseller's sale, a wholesaler's purchase and the same contract's recurring payment must not become three independent profits.

Offers declare currency, charging unit, billing interval, quantity, included allowances, overage tiers, commitments, one-time projects, credits and renewal/exit rules. Physical throughput, named seats, concurrent agents, sessions, minutes, messages, storage and processing each retain their own quantities. Included usage applies to the eligible contract and period before calculating overage. Wholesale costs remain costs even when retail usage is included in a bundle.

## Contract lifecycle

Qualification checks the customer workload, sector, jurisdiction, partner authority and eligible product/deployment versions. Procurement records required evidence and commercial constraints. The player can decline an ineligible bid or propose an allowed alternative. Price cannot compensate for a prohibited administrator, processor or information flow.

An accepted contract creates obligations, service deadlines, support responsibility and billing commitments. Provisioning delays can create project cost, service credits or churn according to explicit terms. Renewal reevaluates current offers and lifecycle status without deleting a previously supported deployment. End of sale, end of renewal, end of support and technical shutdown are separate events.

Staffing costs follow the workforce operator and purchase contract. Implementation engineers consume project capacity independently of support agents. Support incidents consume effort and may escalate across reseller, carrier, platform and specialist boundaries. Acquisitions preserve existing obligations and evidence custody while creating integration projects and consolidated accounts. Financing includes principal, interest, covenants, repayments and default conditions; cash and accounting profit are separate.

## Competition and demand

Fictional messaging, collaboration, conferencing, embedded communications and AI offerings can substitute for one job while complementing another. A meeting tool does not automatically replace PSTN reach or specialist customer service. A bundled suite can reduce willingness to pay for a separate feature while preserving demand for recording, integration or support.

Every market event identifies the demand profile, offer, lifecycle state, relationship or dependency it changes. Players can integrate, rebundle, specialise, improve service or accept reduced demand. They can inspect the cause rather than receive an unexplained revenue haircut. A messaging outage creates delayed or shifted customer needs; a retirement creates a migration project with dates and supported successors.

## Economic acceptance

Test zero usage, exact allowance, overage, shared allowance boundaries, minimum commitment, partial provisioning, cancellation, credit, renewal, acquisition elimination and currency/unit rejection. Repeated rendering or calculation must leave balances unchanged. Replaying the same accepted commands must reproduce every ledger entry. A billed period cannot be billed again after reload or stale worker completion.

The core retains a simple operating-cost diagnostic ledger. The optional `soc-business-1` pack provides a distinct commercial ledger; these balances are displayed separately and must not be added together. Consolidation with the optional SP expansion remains pending.

The business pack implements nine typed offers, supplier/distributor/support roles, scoped expiring grants, minimum quantities, provisioning-dependent activation, wholesale commitments, retail allowances/overage, credits, cancellation liabilities, renewal decisions, loans/interest/principal and acquisition consolidation. Currency is fictional AUD cents and each 60 simulation minutes represents an accelerated billing period. This is explicitly a training interval, not an implied real tariff. Wholesale usage costs apply even inside a retail allowance.

Implementation engineers and support operators are separate from contact-centre people. Projects can block when dependencies fail, then retry. Internal transfers disappear from consolidated income; financing principal changes cash and liabilities but not profit. Market events expose bundle pressure, vendor-direct pressure and offer retirement. Each track requires its service contracts, a billed period, handled demand and fresh service evidence. Initial qualification for government/defence uses synthetic outcome and boundary evidence, not actual accreditation.

The acceptance suite checks allowance boundaries, blocked contracts, credits, renewals, cancellation, financing and acquired groups. Multi-currency accounting, shared cross-contract allowances, covenants/default, complete support escalation and all source-specific offers remain pending.

## Managed customer changes

The optional managed-operations console coordinates bounded joiner and mover changes across independent portfolio customer roots. A joiner adds one canonical identity with optional calling entitlement. A mover grants or revokes one calling entitlement for an existing identity; it is not a generic rename or site move. No-op and unsupported changes fail before submission. Each participant requires an active customer-scoped managed-operation contract, authorized support operator, connector and canonical inventory. One participant's success does not conceal another participant's failure, and a retry targets only that customer's unfinished work once.

An estate job submitting desired configuration is not billable delivery. Settlement requires matching effective generation/configuration, completed provisioning and current observed readiness. Each delivered item posts exactly one `managed-operation` revenue charge (50 AUD cents) and its separate wholesale cost (25 AUD cents) through the actual business ledger. An idempotent retained receipt binds the usage ID, customer, contract, operator, resource and applied generation; no additional charge occurs on inspection or replay. Unfinished or unbilled dispatched work blocks customer deactivation. Completed and posted history remains valid when a customer pauses or the campaign changes track. This model does not claim complete BPO or specialist managed-service coverage.
