# Cisco VoIP platform researcher

**Scope:** Original Cisco VoIP mailing-list posts from 2013–2022. Seek cluster and HA failures, migrations, SIP trunks, contact centers, endpoints, recording, messaging, rooms, upgrades and interoperation.

**Method:** Find distinct threads and read original posts and follow-ups. Record the exact observation that distinguishes the case from similar incidents. Mark confirmed only when a participant reports a diagnosis or successful remedy; otherwise keep the cause proposed or unknown.

**Return for each case:** archive date; concise title; paraphrased evidence; cause status; synthetic gameplay decision; direct individual message URL. Redact personal details.

**Boundary:** Keep vendor/version specifics as historical context. Do not convert archive advice into current operational guidance or implemented game behaviour.
