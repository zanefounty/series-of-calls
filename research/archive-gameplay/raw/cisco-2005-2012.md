# Cisco VoIP archive research (2005–2012)

Each item is an incident seed distilled from an individual archive message. Cause status reflects what the cited thread establishes; proposed diagnoses remain hypotheses.

1. **Date:** 2005-09-14. **Title:** H.323 gateway cannot establish call to CallManager. **Evidence:** Calls disconnect with cause 38 after the gateway attempts two dial peers; responder notes TCP connection to CallManager is likely failing, potentially due to source binding or name resolution. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Compare the gateway’s configured H.323 source address and CM name/IP resolution against the packet path; determine which side fails to establish TCP. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2005-September/007061.html)

2. **Date:** 2005-03-22. **Title:** FXS module repeatedly dies after weeks. **Evidence:** A remote PBX extension crosses a leased-line VoIP path and exits an FXS port into a local PBX; replacement FXS cards reportedly fail after about two weeks. No root cause is stated. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Inspect physical interface conditions, electrical compatibility, call/load patterns, and whether the failure follows the module or chassis. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2005-March/003765.html)

3. **Date:** 2005-05-18. **Title:** Securing phones in a Cisco VoIP deployment. **Evidence:** Archive thread raises securing phones in a system that also runs Unity; the cited item frames a security configuration question rather than a reproduced exploit or confirmed vulnerability. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Choose and validate a phone-security posture, including trust/authentication dependencies and compatibility with voicemail services. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2005-May/005165.html)

4. **Date:** 2005-10-21. **Title:** Call forwarding behavior on a shared line. **Evidence:** A user asks how forwarding behaves on a shared DN; the message raises a call-feature policy problem without establishing a defect. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Reproduce call-forward activation across shared-line appearances and decide whether forwarding should apply per appearance or to the shared DN. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2005-October/007641.html)

5. **Date:** 2006-01-04. **Title:** Unity hears prompts but cannot hear recorded message audio. **Evidence:** PSTN callers reach Unity and hear greetings, but Unity hears silence during recording; internal-originated calls can record. Follow-up reports that changing policy routing from source-based to destination-based solved it. **Cause status:** Confirmed. **Playable investigation:** Compare RTP return paths for internal versus PSTN calls and isolate policy/VPN routing asymmetry. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2006-January/009016.html)

6. **Date:** 2006-01-30. **Title:** New analog gateway ports unexpectedly use translation patterns. **Evidence:** A newly installed analog interface is intended to behave like existing POTS lines, but its calls are affected by translation patterns; the administrator asks how to exempt those ports. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Trace CSS/partition selection per gateway port and decide where digit normalization belongs. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2006-February/009819.html)

7. **Date:** 2007-01-23. **Title:** VG224 analog calls drop and DTMF/hookflash features fail. **Evidence:** Report includes analog-to-analog calls disconnecting when caller hangs up, delayed dial tone after on-hook, hookflash failing for fax forwarding, and users unable to enter banking PINs. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Separate disconnect supervision, hookflash detection, and DTMF relay into independent test cases across the analog call path. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-January/018793.html)

8. **Date:** 2007-01-30. **Title:** Repeated nuisance caller consumes attendant-console lines. **Evidence:** Incoming calls from one source repeatedly occupy multiple attendant-console lines; request is to block or send the caller to voicemail. A response describes an inbound calling-number reject translation as an option. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Decide between gateway-level reject and controlled voicemail diversion; verify caller-ID normalization and avoid blocking legitimate shared numbers. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-January/019012.html)

9. **Date:** 2007-03-01. **Title:** After-hours calls do not route directly to voicemail. **Evidence:** A CCM 4.1(3) administrator says time schedules and partitions are configured but calls outside office hours do not reach voicemail. Reply explains time-of-day partition filtering and gives a routing pattern. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Walk through time zone, schedule periods, partition order, CSS, and voicemail route point using an after-hours test call. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-March/020164.html)

10. **Date:** 2007-03-07. **Title:** Some PRI outbound calls hang while others connect. **Evidence:** `show voice call summary` shows many channels in setup-fail/release states alongside a few connected or waiting channels; the operator asks whether DSP capacity is implicated. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Correlate channel state, Q.931 cause codes, DSP resources, and call concurrency before changing gateway configuration. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-March/020438.html)

11. **Date:** 2007-05-10. **Title:** PSTN callers hear “No circuit/channel available.” **Evidence:** The incident includes Q.931 DISCONNECT signaling with cause 0x8036 for inbound PSTN traffic. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Determine whether the carrier, PRI channel allocation, or gateway capacity is returning the unavailable-channel condition by comparing both sides’ signaling. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-May/022846.html)

12. **Date:** 2007-10-10. **Title:** New CME phones register only after regenerating configuration files. **Evidence:** On CME 4.1 with per-phone configuration and French/English locales, newly added phones fail to register until `create cnf` is run each time. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare generated per-phone XML and TFTP behavior before and after config regeneration; assess template/locale changes. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-October/026688.html)

13. **Date:** 2007-10-15. **Title:** SRST fallback restores a phone’s old extension. **Evidence:** Extension Mobility changes a phone’s DN, but during fallback it returns to an earlier number; resetting the phone and gateway did not clear the behavior. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Check fallback configuration and persisted learned DN state; determine whether the stale number is stored on the gateway or provisioned from another source. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-October/026810.html)

14. **Date:** 2007-10-18. **Title:** Distinguish internal and external call ringing. **Evidence:** A CCM 4.2 administrator wants different ring patterns for internal and external calls; response says feasibility depends on whether calls are handled by a CTI-based auto attendant or Unity transfer path. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Trace the call’s redirect/transfer method and determine where external-call identity survives to the phone. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2007-October/026943.html)

15. **Date:** 2008-01-17. **Title:** Music on hold becomes dead air after several seconds. **Evidence:** CCM 6 callers hear MOH for a few seconds, then silence; resetting the media application restores it temporarily. Packet counters and stream direction are suggested diagnostics. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare RTP packet flow at the phone and media resource before and after the five-second boundary. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-January/029632.html)

16. **Date:** 2008-02-22. **Title:** Unity voicemail is not delivered and MWI stays off. **Evidence:** Unity 5.01 with Domino integration accepts messages, but subscribers see no new message and receive neither email nor MWI; event log reports UMR delivery failure due to configuration/connectivity with partner mail server. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Verify Unity message repository delivery, partner mail connectivity, subscriber mailbox state, and MWI signaling independently. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-February/030907.html)

17. **Date:** 2008-03-12. **Title:** Emergency-call alerting and location monitoring requirements. **Evidence:** Administrators discuss how to alert staff when a 911 call is placed and whether the system can expose location/callback information or enable monitoring. This is a design gap, not a reported malfunction. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Define the operational response and validate that emergency call location, callback number, and alert reach the right staff. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-March/031711.html)

18. **Date:** 2008-04-22. **Title:** CME outbound route sends access digit to the PSTN. **Evidence:** Internal callers dial 9 for an outside call, and the outbound POTS dial peer forwards all digits without stripping the access digit. **Cause status:** Confirmed. **Playable investigation:** Trace digit transformations from phone to dial peer and choose where the outside-line prefix is removed. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-April/033279.html)

19. **Date:** 2008-06-19. **Title:** Inbound FXO calls ring but never reach the attendant DN. **Evidence:** Calls arrive through an MGCP-controlled FXO port from a GSM-to-analog gateway; outbound calls work, but inbound signaling progresses through ring states and then hangs up. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Follow ring supervision, answer supervision, and MGCP call setup from the analog device to the configured attendant DN. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-June/034984.html)

20. **Date:** 2008-09-03. **Title:** Migrated phones cannot find CME firmware/configuration. **Evidence:** During a move from CallManager to CME, phones report hostname lookup failure, no DNS server, and file-not-found messages; router TFTP/SCCP debug shows no matching requests. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Verify DHCP option 150, phone-stored server settings, DNS, TFTP file inventory, and whether phones are contacting the expected host. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-September/037760.html)

21. **Date:** 2008-11-13. **Title:** Cluster upgrade staging and reboot sequencing. **Evidence:** An administrator asks whether publisher and subscriber inactive partitions can both be upgraded while call processing continues and rebooted later, rather than upgrading/rebooting nodes sequentially. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Build a maintenance decision around cluster upgrade sequencing, compatibility, redundancy, and acceptable outage windows. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2008-November/039769.html)

22. **Date:** 2009-05-05. **Title:** Internal call abruptly plays “cannot be completed as dialed.” **Evidence:** After a year of working, two registered phones at different sites cannot call each other; the prompt is identified as CallManager annunciator, and the call is not routed to a voice gateway. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Inspect CUCM call routing, line state, CSS/partition visibility, and bandwidth/location admission for a local intersite call. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2009-May/000984.html)

23. **Date:** 2009-09-29. **Title:** VG224 SCCP gateway intermittently fails to register. **Evidence:** A gateway port is sometimes unregistered; the eventual follow-up reveals the investigated identifier belonged to an unused, unconfigured FXS port, while the original intermittent fault was no longer reproducible. The thread also considers mixed-mode security and transient unregister defects. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Establish a reproducible failure and compare successful/failed SCCP registration traces while verifying each virtual port’s CUCM configuration. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2009-September/005576.html)

24. **Date:** 2009-11-09 (resolved 2009-11-16). **Title:** CUCM 6.1 to 7.0 upgrade fails with generic file-access error. **Evidence:** Upgrade fails after about 45 minutes from DVD and FTP with an unknown upgrade-file error; follow-up says the restored lab system lacked a Nokia S60 device option package present in production, and installing it allowed the upgrade. **Cause status:** Confirmed. **Playable investigation:** Compare device-option packages and locale inventories between the source and restored lab before retrying an upgrade. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2009-November/007285.html)

25. **Date:** 2010-02-18. **Title:** H.323 call setup fails after a TCP connection timeout. **Evidence:** Gateway debug shows cause 38 after roughly three seconds trying the outgoing dial peer; responder interprets timing as a dropped/black-holed TCP SYN to the remote H.323 service. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Test reachability to the configured H.323 signaling endpoint and inspect ACL/firewall routing for silent drops versus explicit rejects. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2010-February/010266.html)

26. **Date:** 2010-11-12. **Title:** SIP trunk call has one-way audio after blind transfer. **Evidence:** Incoming trunk calls work directly, but an IP phone transferring to an off-net party without consultation leaves the remote party hearing audio while the phone hears none; thread debates SDP/RTP path and MTP behavior. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Compare pre-transfer and post-transfer SDP, RTP addresses/counters, and media flow-through/flow-around behavior. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2010-November/018198.html)

27. **Date:** 2010-12-14. **Title:** UC540 calls work one direction between SCCP and SIP phones. **Evidence:** SIP phones can call Cisco phones and voicemail, but Cisco-to-SIP calls do not ring; SIP calls to outside destinations report busy while Cisco outbound works. Registration counters show SIP phones registered. **Cause status:** Unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare dial-peer matching and SIP response behavior in both directions, then inspect the external route’s called-number format. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2010-December/018710.html)

28. **Date:** 2011-08-13. **Title:** ITSP calls ring through instead of forwarding to CUE voicemail. **Evidence:** External calls through an ITSP SIP trunk keep ringing instead of forwarding to CUE; voicemail works when dialed directly. A reply proposes adding B2BUA on the voicemail dial peer. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Check forwarded-call signaling and whether the provider/CUE call leg preserves the redirect semantics expected by the voicemail peer. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2011-August/023392.html)

29. **Date:** 2011-11-11. **Title:** H.323 call connects with audio, then drops after five seconds. **Evidence:** Calls between PBXs through two gatekeeper-controlled routers connect with two-way audio for five seconds and then disconnect; responder points to gatekeeper monitoring/disconnect behavior as a possibility. **Cause status:** Proposed. **Playable investigation:** Correlate gatekeeper RAS messages, H.225/H.245 teardown, and disconnect cause at the five-second mark. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2011-November/025086.html)

30. **Date:** 2012-08-10 (analysis posted 2012-08-13). **Title:** UCCX inbound calls fail while script appears to continue. **Evidence:** UCCX 7 logs show inbound calls failing with cause 107 during CTI Port answer; Cisco responder identifies media negotiation failure with the CTI Port and notes the script may appear to continue despite the failed call. **Cause status:** Confirmed (media negotiation failure identified). **Playable investigation:** Compare CTI Port media/resource negotiation with a successful call and determine whether script handling masks the failed call leg. **Source:** [message](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2012-August/029363.html)
