# Jurisdiction and fictional information boundaries

Jurisdiction packs separate real research claims from fictional scenario rules. A saved exercise pins both versions. Research observations do not rewrite an existing exercise. A new accepted review can produce a new pack and an explicit migration option; old scenarios remain reproducible.

The foundation uses `fictional-boundaries-1`: AU, US, JP or EU selects the allowed region for recording, archive and transcription. Government lessons require recording, at least thirty fictional days of retention, supervisor access and in-region transcription. Defence lessons require specified local calling/meeting dependencies during isolation. These are authored game contracts, not statements that a country universally requires domestic processing or a thirty-day retention period.

## Required full profile dimensions

Each contract identifies workload, sensitivity, allowed product edition/version, storage and backup locations, processing/media/support locations, administrator identities and locations, privileged-access conditions, key custody, connectivity, continuity, maintenance and permitted information flows. Information includes messages, audio/video, recordings, transcripts, metadata, logs, backups and indexes. A local storage choice cannot satisfy a restriction on remote processing or administrative access.

The profile separates mandatory eligibility from preferences. An ineligible mandatory dependency blocks the bid or feature; preference weights can affect scoring without masquerading as legal requirements. Customer-specific restrictions can be stricter than general source guidance and must be labelled as scenario requirements.

## Regional research boundaries

Australia needs scoped research on assessment, authorisation, shared responsibility, procurement and relevant information-handling frameworks. An assessment is evidence about the assessed service and scope; it must not be treated as a universal vendor approval. US profiles distinguish commercial and government cloud environments, the workload's applicable assessment/authorisation route and the agency's own decision. Japanese profiles need dated service-list and procurement applicability rather than assuming every product from a listed supplier is eligible. EU-wide profiles must distinguish information-transfer rules from a universal domestic-hosting requirement and must not imply a member-state or defence procurement approval.

Five initial jurisdiction observations and 23 historical operator-report observations are recorded as separate stable source items in `research/additions.json` and linked into the generated source, claim, reference and requirement registers. Product-model observations remain in `research/observations.json`. None of the added observations activates gameplay or makes a legal claim. Active jurisdiction claims require a reviewer-accepted source, applicability, uncertainty and acceptance link, with a review date no older than ninety days. Product lifecycle notices likewise need the relevant offer, region and version rather than a brand-wide expiry date.

## Disconnection tests

Isolation disables external dependencies by function. Local call/media capability, local directory, local token validation, DNS, time, certificates, licence state, recording and processing each have independent evidence. A licence grace period must be explicit, timed and saved. Disconnected operation does not imply permission to export accumulated records later. Reconnection checks allowed destinations, replay/deduplication and recovery ordering.

The foundation tests local identity/DNS/time/certificate/licensing booleans and an external-licence dependency for local speech processing. It is an introductory dependency exercise. Real protocol expiry, licence grace, external management, update supply and disconnected recovery queues remain pending.
