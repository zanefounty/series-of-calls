## Consolidated owner review notes

1. **Keep product dimensions separate.** Microsoft’s live documentation supports distinct PSTN operating models, room licensing, meeting/event formats, contact-centre integration patterns, and compliance-recording limits. Feature matrices, plan availability, cloud scope, device firmware, and eligibility are changeable or specific; preserve those qualifiers in any accepted wording. In particular, Microsoft 365 Local documents listed server products on Azure Local but does not itself prove the broader “Teams is never on-premises” formulation.

2. **Split time-sensitive lifecycle statements by service and event.** The ACS guide distinguishes standalone retirement from services that remain with breaking changes, and its onboarding restriction has its own effective date. Cisco EOL15972 and EOL15916 are dated notices with affected SKU sets and different order, renewal/change, and support dates. These dated notices can support their listed lifecycle events; they should not be generalized to unrelated SKUs or services.

3. **Treat vendor capability and marketplace pages as scoped assertions.** Webex suite, Flex, Dedicated Instance, Contact Center, Connect, interoperability, Wholesale RTM, App Hub, and partner product pages support the specific product descriptions reviewed. They do not establish exhaustive feature parity, certification, legal suitability, regional availability, or a customer’s entitlement. The WhatsApp pricing page describes delivered-message rates varying by category and market and conditional free service/utility messages; rates are mutable.

4. **Preserve architecture and licensing boundaries.** Cisco AI PODs describe local processing and isolated deployment, but explicitly make licensing an exception to zero external connectivity and tie integrations to versions. Microsoft government cloud pages require eligibility validation. These are not customer approval determinations.

5. **Lifecycle-management examples remain illustrative.** Kurmi, VOSS, Akkadian, VQ, Mividas, Pexip, Synergy SKY, and IR sources were reviewed as relevant examples, but they are supplier materials with differing scope and evidence detail. The recommendation does not infer common functionality, comparative performance, or a validated product selection.
