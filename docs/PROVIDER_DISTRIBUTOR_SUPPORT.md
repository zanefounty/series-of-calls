# Earned distributor-linked support tariff

This version-8 Provider choice uses the existing `provider-support-coverage` purchase with `coverageId: distributor-extended`. It requires an earned `progress.progression-level.7` receipt for the target market and that customer's current qualified paid distributor-room relationship. It is a commercial price condition: the named Orchard MSP employee still supplies support. The distributor's existing qualification scopes remain room supply and recording; no new labour or legal authority is implied.

The tariff costs 24,000 cents setup and 12,000 cents per period, with eight minutes of implementation and one shared support slot. Ordinary extended coverage costs 18,000 setup and 16,000 per period with six minutes. Both provide the same all-day shift, named person, language and two-customer maximum; neither creates staff or customer agents. At a quoted three-period minimum, totals are 60,000 versus 66,000 cents before shared payroll and other service commitments.

The contextual comparison shows the locked tariff and explains its prerequisites. `canOrder(state, request, hooks)` checks the actual customer/market and returns a pure `earnedTerms` snapshot. Purchase stores `project.supportCommercialEvidence = {version:1, sourceId, receiptId, allowedMarkets, acceptedAt, distributor}`. The new tariff's operation receipt adds `commercial`, containing the same fields plus initially null `deliveredAt` and `deliveryDistributor`. Existing coverage receipt shapes remain unchanged.

`completeEligibility(state, project, hooks)` does not mutate state. It requires a currently qualified distributor and returns `commissionEvidence` for the coordinator's actual completion callback. A loss before delivery leaves the project commissioning; it does not invent eligibility. A loss after delivery makes current support ineligible while committed charges and historical observations remain. A paid qualification renewal can restore current eligibility. Historical checks pin the purchase and completion snapshots to their actual minutes and paid project/posting lineage, rather than requiring today's certificate to match an old one.

The responsibility helper separates the original room purchase permission from current qualification in version 8. It derives the purchase certificate from a retained paid completed qualification whose valid interval covered the room order. A renewed certificate is checked at the present or sampled minute and is never backdated to the original purchase. Existing snapshot keys and older campaign behavior remain unchanged.

Allowed same-person replacements are business-hours→extended, business-hours→distributor-extended, extended→distributor-extended, and distributor-extended→extended. The ordinary tariff provides an escape from the qualification-dependent offer. Replacement retains prior minimum charges and history; one pending replacement does not allocate a second copy of the same named person. Quote validation, cash/staff reservations and billing remain owned by the normal Provider dispatcher.

## Integration and verification scope

The pure regional helper and renderer are implemented with explicit purchase/completion validation. The coordinator owns authored coverage terms, earned unlock descriptor, quote/project fields, completion callback, translations and shared restore validation. New translation keys are supplied in `/tmp/provider-distributor-support-labels.json`.

`tests/provider-distributor-support.test.js` exercises actual public unearned refusal and the visible locked comparison, plus paid distributor expiry/requalification using the existing room. Its fixtures do not fabricate an earned L7 receipt. The regional helper/UI regression suite also checks unchanged ordinary coverage and the third correctly scoped locked offer. A genuine five-market L7 claim followed by paid tariff delivery, expiry recovery, worker/restore and visible purchase remains required before the complete follow-on is accepted. No helper fixture or passing count substitutes for that public path.

This is a bounded fictional progression adaptation. T2 integration-aware assurance, the canonical endgame, broader distributor catalogue behavior and external research acceptance remain separate work.

Development results: the existing regional helper and renderer suite passed 18/18 (`/tmp/soc-distributor-support-existing-r2.log`). The two new public regressions passed 2/2 in 58 seconds (`/tmp/soc-distributor-support-public-r2.log`). The original failed run is preserved at `/tmp/soc-distributor-support-public.log`: the regional snapshot read the qualification without the observation hooks used for its responsibility, producing a null certificate at minute 134. Passing the same trusted hooks fixes that mismatch; historical validation was not relaxed. The original lawful minute-133 state now advances and restores exactly. These are development results; full earned L7 tariff delivery still requires its genuine five-market fixture.
