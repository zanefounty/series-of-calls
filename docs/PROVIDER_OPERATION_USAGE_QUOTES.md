# Provider operation usage-rate disclosure

Operation quotes previously displayed “Not quoted” for variable usage even though actual customer work, including owned-site recovery calls, incurred the selected customer's plan rates. Purchase quotes already displayed those rates.

`SOC.provider.quote` now supplies the customer's existing voice-minute and delivered-message rates for an operation in both the commercial terms and forecast. The existing comparison renderer displays them. This does not change charges, purchase terms, fixed operation commitments, eligibility or the atomic dispatch boundary. An unaffordable operation still shows its rates while commitment refuses.

AC-SOC-640–643 cover real paid Swift and Steady openings, pure quotes and rendering, actual settled units multiplied by the disclosed rates, unchanged purchase and operation fixed terms, insufficient-cash refusal, and customer scope. Both published plans currently have the same unit rates. A separate pure-kernel fixture deliberately supplies different authored rates to detect accidental reuse of another customer's tariff; it is not dispatched or presented as campaign balance or delivery evidence.

This bounded disclosure repair does not complete every operation-cost assertion or broader ecosystem acceptance. Final candidate and visible-control results are recorded after execution; no human playability evidence is claimed.
