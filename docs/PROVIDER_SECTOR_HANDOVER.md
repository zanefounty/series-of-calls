# R13-08: next-sector portfolio handover

The 13 November audit observed a sector switch while the previous expansion was still provisioning and the opening customer awaited renewal. The delivered-work victory rule now prevents that exact early award, but the current transition still hides the live portfolio workload. This contract adds a quoted handover for fresh visible Provider careers. Existing saves without `sectorHandoverVersion: 1` keep their recorded direct transition path.

At an earned victory, the player may continue operating the current portfolio or review entry to the next sector. The review lists every current customer's status, pending projects and incidents, existing fixed supplier exposure, shared cash, and the setup, period and staffing requirements of both next-sector plans. It shows cash after each possible full-plan setup without spending it. The player chooses to retain the current provider team or hire one engineer and one support person. Hiring uses the same validated staff command, increases real payroll and capacity, and is recorded with the sector handover. Neither option cancels existing customer obligations or buys the next customer's plan automatically. The next-sector order still needs its own quote and funds.

The review and decision are revision-bound Provider commands. A fresh career cannot enter the next sector through the old direct action. The accepted handover records the won mission proof, current customer IDs, cash and workforce before and after the choice, and the selected staffing path. Inspection must be pure. Saves and workers must preserve the record and reject invented choices or changed history. A failed or stale quote leaves the portfolio unchanged.

Acceptance requires: a delivered expansion and real prior obligations; both quotes and their different payroll/capacity effects; a low-cash case whose next purchase remains unavailable after the handover; blocked early or forged transitions; save/worker parity; and a visible browser choice. The version 2 delegated path below adds the contracted alternative. Uncoached comprehension remains separate work.

## Version 2: scoped workforce delegation

R13-08's contract choice uses the existing fictional People Orchard BPO operation for one named active customer at the moment of sector handover. It delegates that customer's routine handling capacity, not the MSP's customer contract, treasury, renewal decisions, incident repair or supplier obligations. The contract costs A$300 setup immediately and A$180 per period while billable, provisions two BPO agents only after real commissioning, and adds no carrier sessions. The handover quote shows the named customer, setup and recurring cost, commissioning delay, post-contract cash and both next-sector plan affordability forecasts. The player may instead retain the team or hire one engineer and one support person. All three paths keep the existing portfolio. A blocked BPO order or stale quote cannot advance the sector.

Fresh visible careers opt into `sectorHandoverVersion: 2`. Version 1 careers and saves retain their prior retain/reinforce choice and receipt schema. Version 2 records the BPO project and ledger posting in the handover, linked to the ordinary paid operation; save and Worker validation reject invented contracts. Positive acceptance requires a delivered expansion, an eligible customer, paid BPO commissioning and subsequent real handling capacity. Negative acceptance requires blocked duplicate/unaffordable orders, a stale quote and forged handover references. This bounded delegation does not imply a full third-party portfolio manager; uncoached comprehension remains required.
