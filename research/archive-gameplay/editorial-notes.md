# Editorial notes for the 75-case catalogue

The three researchers produced 83 individually linked candidates: 30 Cisco VoIP cases from 2005–2012, 28 from 2013–2022, and 25 VoiceOps cases. The source editor removed eight from the curated catalogue:

- Cisco early 3: a broad phone-security question without a specific service symptom.
- Cisco early 4: a shared-line forwarding question with too little evidence to distinguish a case.
- Cisco early 21: general cluster upgrade staging discussion.
- Cisco early 25: repeats the H.323 TCP setup mechanism in Cisco early 1.
- Cisco late 7: overlaps the earlier blind-transfer one-way-audio investigation.
- VoiceOps 12: switch-selection procurement discussion rather than an incident.
- VoiceOps 17: rate-deck question with thin game evidence.
- VoiceOps 24: capture-workflow question overlaps stronger observability cases.

The retained catalogue includes some design decisions and open investigations because the user requested *possible use cases*. Each such entry is marked as a proposal; none is a claim that the historical operator found a final cause. Case IDs are local to this research folder and are not `REQ-`, `AC-` or implemented-behaviour identifiers.
