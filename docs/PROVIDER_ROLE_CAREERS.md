# Customer IT and MSP career contract

The 13 November 2026 gameplay audit R13-09 and the critical review's fifth gameplay finding require two playable perspectives before the proposed six-role catalogue expands. The current Provider campaign always creates `orchard-msp` as the player. Its inherited-estate opening funds a service but leaves the same MSP authority, contracts and margin objective. It is therefore an MSP variant, not a Customer IT career.

This document defines the first bounded career pair. It applies the organisation, ownership, delegation and contract distinctions in [the domain specification](../spec/02-domain.md). All organisations, contracts, outages and money are synthetic. The broader source adaptation and the four later roles remain pending.

## Career identity and first decision

| | Customer IT | MSP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player organisation | The customer organisation, with its own inherited estate | Orchard Communications, operating under a scoped customer grant |
| Initial assets and liability | Existing sites, requirements, internal budget, staff and service continuity | Customer contracts, supplier commitments, payroll, operating margin and support response |
| First consequential choice | Allocate a limited internal budget to protect one site or fund the first migration step; either choice leaves a different exposure to the first outage | Choose the opening customer promise and funded supplier/capacity plan; either choice changes recurring margin and the outage response options |
| Authority | May change customer-owned estate and workforce within explicit policy; may accept or revoke a service delegation. Cannot spend the MSP's treasury, sell on its behalf, or silently alter supplier commitments. | May spend its own treasury and meet customer contracts. May change customer assets only within the active grant; cannot control customer workforce or revoke the customer's grant. |
| Success | The inherited service remains continuous through the outage and the chosen site migration delivers within the internal budget and recorded customer requirement | Delivered customer work meets the promise after the outage, obligations remain covered, and assessed operating result is positive |

The first decision must affect a real paid/effective resource, work schedule or exposure before the outage. A displayed role name or different prose does not satisfy this contract. The role is immutable for a career save. A new role marker is versioned; old saves retain their recorded MSP behavior.

## Implemented opening slice

The visible Enterprise new-game form can start a Customer IT career for Cedar Services. It creates a separate A$9,000 internal budget and two customer-owned sites while retaining Orchard Communications as a distinct MSP treasury. The first revision-bound quote lets Customer IT spend A$3,500 to protect North access in three minutes or A$5,000 to migrate South to a managed platform in four minutes. At minute eight, the same scheduled North access outage is protected or interrupts service based on the effective protection state. An interrupted North site can be repaired for A$2,000 and two minutes of work. Internal postings, effective sites and observed lost minutes persist through save and Worker replay; cross-role commands are rejected. Legacy MSP careers are unaffected.

This is an opening slice only. It does not yet supply the shared MSP contract, customer and MSP acceptance of a multi-site migration, common work and outage settlement, role-specific victory or full browser careers. The Customer IT budget and site observations must be carried into those later stages rather than replaced by a fresh generic estate.

The [desktop and 390px Chromium opening journeys](../evidence/gameplay-audit-2026-11-13/customer-it-opening/README.md) cover both choices, the outage, paid repair, keyboard approval, save and reload with zero source drift. They do not establish the later shared migration or victory requirements.

## Shared contract and convergence

The two careers encounter the same named multi-site migration brief and technical outage. A shared contract identifies customer buyer, MSP seller, service operator and support owner independently. It records the customer requirement and internal spend cap, the MSP retail and supplier terms, customer-owned assets, delegated change scope, escalation and expiry, plus separate customer and MSP acceptance of the migration window. Commissioning and any rollback carry those parties and obligations forward; segment transition must not replace them.

The Customer IT player can select sites, approve requirements and change windows, spend internal funds and inspect whether the MSP delivered. The MSP player can quote, fund supplier capacity and implement only the approved sites and window. Each can inspect the other party's obligations, but dispatch rejects the other party's commands. When the common outage hits, the customer faces continuity loss and internal remediation cost while the MSP faces service credits, supplier cost and margin. The same observed service evidence feeds both assessments without inventing a second outage result.

The convergence must preserve the opening site's actual effective configuration, cash/ledger, outstanding work, grant and accepted contract. A role-specific projection may explain these differently, but inspection and rendering are pure. Save validation must reject changed party, authority, budget, accepted scope or historical outcome. Worker replay and import use the same validated SOC command boundary.

## Required proof before calling R13-09 implemented

1. Start each career through the visible new-game flow and make its first choice using a quote and dispatched command. Show a different cash posting, effective resource or accepted obligation, then settle real work.
2. Trigger the same synthetic outage in both careers. Demonstrate distinct customer continuity/budget and MSP contract/margin consequences from the same service facts.
3. Reach the shared multi-site migration from both openings. Show that accepted sites, window, grant, contract and inherited liabilities survive convergence, including a blocked unauthorised cross-role command.
4. Test stale quotes, insufficient funds, expired/revoked grants, incomplete work, rollback, forged saves and deterministic worker replay. Retain desktop and 390px browser journeys, including save/reload and an accessibility pass.
5. Register source-linked behavior and acceptance cases, regenerate evidence, and run the relevant behavioral, gate mutation, translation and build checks. Keep R13-09 and SOC-09 open until both careers and this proof exist. The remaining four role designs require their own authority, income, scarce resource and victory evidence before any six-career claim.
