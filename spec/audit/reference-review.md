# Reference review (2026-11-03)

## Scope and method

This review checks the 68 registered URLs in `research/generated/references.json` against their linked assertions in `research/generated/claims.json` and the preserved design. Each record in [`reference-review-2026-11-03.json`](../../research/reference-review-2026-11-03.json) retains the stable reference ID, original URL, checked date, page title, access result, evidence type, claim IDs, scoped finding, and the URL actually opened.

The registered pages were opened in the browser in the requested order: the five jurisdiction sources, the product and integration sources, then the pinned upstream source references. Conclusions are limited to what the opened page states. A product page or partner listing is recorded as supplier/marketplace evidence, not independent verification or certification. Lifecycle, licensing, prices, catalog entries and feature availability can change.

Four pinned upstream GitHub references (REF-267d4dda6bb79608 and REF-01c1db3086024441 through REF-31e291b1eee75a7a) could not be retrieved: the browser returned a cache miss for the repository and each exact file URL, and an exact-page search did not find them. They remain in the audit as unresolved historical references. No assertions about their content were inferred from local copies.

## Findings that materially narrow the design claims

- **Jurisdiction sources support conditional assessment, not blanket approval.** The Australian cloud guidance describes risk-based assessment, shared responsibility and ongoing assurance. FedRAMP explicitly leaves each agency responsible for authorizing its own system, configuration and integrations. ISMAP registration is a procurement reference and does not replace AI-specific risk review. EDPB transfer guidance describes transfer criteria and mechanisms; it does not establish an EU-wide data-localization requirement.
- **Microsoft product and integration behavior is profile-specific.** Teams PSTN modes, Rooms licenses, event types, contact-center integration models, Direct Routing certification, government cloud availability and compliance recording are distinct. Certified SBC support is tied to listed products/firmware. Microsoft 365 Local’s opened overview lists Exchange Server, SharePoint Server and Skype for Business Server on Azure Local, but does not explicitly say “no on-premises Teams edition”; that negative assertion remains unresolved from this URL.
- **Current lifecycle claims need dated snapshots.** The opened Cisco notices state UCM Cloud for Government last order on 2027-01-06 and renewal/support through 2028-01-31; Webex WFO last order on 2026-07-25, renewal through 2027-07-30 and support through 2031-07-31. ACS documentation states the standalone retirement date as 2028-09-30 and distinguishes feature-specific transitions. Preserve the exact product/offer and separate end-of-sale, renewal, and support dates.
- **Vendor and marketplace pages substantiate product claims only at their own scope.** Cisco/Webex, UC management, conferencing, CCaaS, WEM, speech and meeting-assistant sources describe different products and prerequisites. App Hub presence is not formal certification. Supplier claims about air-gapped operation, security, scale, compliance or uptime remain supplier assertions unless supported by separate evidence.
- **Meeting assistants add identity and data paths.** Otter’s page says the Notetaker appears as a guest and the meeting tenant must allow guests; Read AI’s Teams integration can sync channel content and may need admin approval. Their own setup requirements demonstrate why bot presence, tenant policy, content access and consent should remain explicit dependencies.
- **Offline AI has explicit operating requirements.** Deepgram’s self-hosted option is limited to Premium customers and makes the customer responsible for infrastructure, backups and monitoring. Microsoft’s disconnected containers require approval and commitment-tier billing; their offline usage records still need collection. This supports separating local processing from disconnected licensing and administrative dependencies.

## Review disposition

The JSON uses per-reference dispositions such as `Supported`, `Supported with scope limits`, `Partial`, and `Unresolved`; these are audit findings, not accepted claims or legal/product approvals. The most important outstanding source gap is the four inaccessible pinned GitHub URLs. Several claims are supported only by broad marketing or navigation pages and should remain at category/profile level until an exact technical or licensing source is attached. The Microsoft 365 Local negative claim also needs a direct primary source before it is stated as fact.

A later governance review can decide which narrow assertions are eligible for acceptance. This audit itself changes no source registry status and does not assert that any jurisdiction, certification, or product configuration is approved.
