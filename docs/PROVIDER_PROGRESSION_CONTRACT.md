## Adapter boundary

Add a separate versioned Provider progression record and pure evaluator, using the same canonical source IDs and names. Feed it validated facts from `providerCampaign.customers`, projects and setup postings, actual job-to-interaction lineage, meeting sessions, migrations, incidents, customer histories, consolidated period histories, and current effective resource/deployment generations. Reuse existing domain proof evaluators where their real prerequisites exist; add explicit adapters for central agreements and settlement instead of pretending they are tenant business contracts.

An earned receipt must contain its content version, source ID, customer/tenant scope, evidence interval, referenced project/job/interaction/settlement IDs, relevant generations, and any typed proof summary needed after bounded retention. Never use an unvalidated boolean, purchased product name, completed project alone, lifetime throughput peak, or inspector selection as delivered evidence. Probes, Lab work and Training awards cannot earn Provider progression.

Claim/unlock application enters `SOC.dispatch`, rechecks current eligibility atomically and issues a once-only receipt. Rendering and preview are pure. Retain historical achievement evidence after a later outage, but suspend offers that require current eligibility. Restore must validate proofs and reject unknown content versions, forged receipts, stale-generation substitutions and duplicate rewards. Old saves gain no invented historical achievements.

Unlocks should expose a costed choice: another eligible contract, changed supplier terms, an additional policy scope or a newly available operating method. A goal display or badge alone does not meet PLAY-027. Existing starting services must remain purchasable; no unlock may depend on buying the same locked capability. Each proposed unlock needs an authored offer/operation, cost, lead time, staff requirements and an actual dispatch consequence before it is considered implemented.

In the tables, **available** means a current data source or operation exists, not that the proposed complete predicate passes. **Missing** includes the adapter and visible acceptance even where a domain pack already has a partial proof.

## Canonical endgame and eligibility

Preserve the physical game's endgame unchanged: `GOALS.t1.win` delegates to `isTier1()` in [80-hot-ports-and-rank.js](../src/game/70-rules/80-hot-ports-and-rank.js:20): every save-version-required Tier 1 has an active PNI, no transit ports remain, and measured reach exceeds 0.999. `t1Need()` preserves older saves' required peer set. Level thirteen is not this win predicate.

For communications, preserve the existing source-linked `progress.goal.t1` sustainable-operator meaning. It is not a literal claim of carrier Tier 1 status. The current domain qualification in [36-market-qualification.js](../src/game/36-market-qualification.js:20) specifies four segment roots, fresh `rd` continuity, new external bills and positive operating profit over 60 minutes. A Provider adapter must meet those obligations using actual Provider records or leave the goal blocked with explicit missing evidence. Do not collapse it into “four missions won,” “level thirteen reached,” or “all products bought.”

The minimum honest implementation sequence is: central agreement/settlement evidence adapter; real first sustainable customer and next paid opportunity; multi-tenant proof and bounded provisioning unlock; then capability-specific paid controls that produce the missing integration/site/security/continuity proofs. Display all 32 identities from the start with measured reasons, but claim only those with complete executable predicates and consequential unlocks. This display is progress visibility, not completion of blocked rows.

Verification must include per-ID successful, missing/forged/stale evidence and recovery cases; no reward from probes or Training; once-only dispatch and save/worker equivalence; retained historical evidence after pruning; current eligibility loss; actual use of each unlocked choice; and a canonical endgame counterexample with one missing segment, invalid continuity or non-operating money. No registry or research status changes follow from this proposal.
