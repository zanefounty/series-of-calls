# First-expansion prospect choice

Scope: the 13 November 2026 gameplay audit finding R13-03 and critical-review finding SOC-08. This is a bounded synthetic SMB campaign mechanic, not a completed sales or portfolio simulation.

The first qualifying period opens two leads at one persisted `offerOpenedAt`. The regular `next-1` lead retains the selected opening workload and expires 180 simulation minutes after opening. `next-1-a` offers a different authored SMB workload and retail ceiling and expires 150 minutes after opening. For a narrow launch, the alternative is the peak calling workload at A$300 more per period before market adjustments. For a peak opening, the alternative is the standard workload at A$200 less. Both offers have independent visible quote buttons, complete plan comparisons, workload summaries and fixed expiry times. Losing the earlier offer leaves the regular lead available until its own deadline; seeking a replacement is an explicit validated command after the regular lead expires.

Fresh SMB careers with an explicit opening promise set `prospectChoiceVersion: 1`. Accepted alternative customers store `offerId` and `prospectDemandId`. The customer-specific demand controls actual arrivals, durations, deadlines, incident authority, usage forecasts and worker replay. Funding still uses the existing `plan` or `purchase` command, posts actual setup cost, commissions all three stages, and requires delivered work before the mission can be won. `inspect` and UI rendering are read-only. Imported saves reject a forged prospect version, demand choice, customer name or retail agreement. Earlier saves without the version marker retain their original single lead and remain restorable.

Acceptance cases `AC-SOC-885`–`AC-SOC-888` cover simultaneous terms and expiry, accepted demand through dispatch/save/worker, two profitable settled portfolio strategies, and legacy/forgery behavior. The mobile browser journey `prospect-peak-mobile` additionally accepts the alternative through the rendered Deals comparison and observes effective stages and delivered work. The narrow Swift and peak Steady mobile journeys inspect both visible offers and preserve the existing career path.

Later portfolio cycles and the non-SMB segments still use their previous one-lead design. Retention, churn, broader market reactions and the uncoached human pilot remain open in the audit worklist.
