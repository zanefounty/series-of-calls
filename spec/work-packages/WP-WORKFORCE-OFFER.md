# Proposed Queueberry workforce offer

This bounded implementation covers part of `uc-new:actor:genesys` (`REQ-98ba1bedb8589fa1`) and `uc-new:domain:workforce` (`REQ-ef684c98bb36340a`). `BEH-SOC-378` and `AC-SOC-498`–`530` register its executable contracts. Both source obligations remain partial. Queueberry is the existing proposed fictional alias, still requiring owner review; its version, features, prices and supplier observations are synthetic training data. There is no original source comparison for this new actor; existing narrative comparisons are unchanged.

The offer provides named workforce-management forecast coverage. It does not buy contact-routing seats, create people or change their skills, shifts, attendance or concurrency. A manual workforce configuration remains a valid alternative. One WFM-covered person forecasts capacity of 15 voice interactions per hour in the example, while the independently staffed two-person contact service forecasts 30 and can actually handle two simultaneous calls.

The player orders `queueberry-wfm-standard` version `1.0`, provisions an integration bound to the same tenant and the effective `people` resource, records supplier/application observations, runs a test, and binds real roster members to that exact test. A supplier failure records a failed result. Repair requires a new observation and test; an older pass cannot revive from another positive observation, even within the same simulation minute. Resource changes invalidate current coverage until explicit retesting and rebinding, while historical evidence survives.

Activation takes one simulation minute. Each effective agent unit costs a synthetic 4,200 AUD cents per prepaid 60 training minutes. Amendments and cancellation take effect at the next paid boundary, retaining the current service and invoice until then. Restart receives a new invoice ID. Offer costs and transport costs reconcile independently against the business ledger; genuine ledger eviction retains its pruning marker. Viewing a forecast or retrying a command does not bill twice.

Forecasts use duration-qualified arrival observations and eligible named people, with their actual skills, language, shifts, observed availability and concurrency. Mean forecasting weights total arrivals by total sample duration; last-period forecasting uses only its latest contributing observation. WFM coverage is separate from actual call assignment, which uses the existing workforce dispatcher. Customer-controlled staffing rejects vendor administration; BPO administration needs a current delegation.

The workforce-offer console uses the shared dispatcher for ordering, integration configuration, observations, tests, binding and forecasting. Read-only inspection is exposed through Calls MCP. Saves validate tenant, version, generations, test/observation/binding lineage, chronology, paid-period boundaries and cross-pack invoices. Worker execution uses the same kernel. These checks establish structural consistency of a synthetic save, not cryptographic authenticity.

The acceptance journeys cover failed integration and recovery, a valid manual alternative, actual two-agent calling, duration-weighted forecasts, absence and return, expired delegation and renewal, delayed cancellation, restart, transport coexistence and imported-state rejection. Wider contact-centre offers, CRM/Teams integration, quality evaluation, full BPO planning and complete source-specific actor fidelity remain outstanding. The actor is not complete merely because this offer exists.
