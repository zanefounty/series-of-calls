# Paid Provider meeting choices

Content `provider-campaigns-5` adds an authored browser guest room alongside the native room and paid Cisco interoperability recipe. Prices are fictional scenario balance in cents, not vendor offers.

| Choice | Setup | Per period | Work | Total healthy delivery | Participants | Capture/captions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Browser guest | 24000 | 6000 | 4 min | 4 min | 2 | Unsupported |
| Native room | 75000 | 9000 | 10 min | 10 min | 8 | Supported with separately funded dependencies |
| Interoperability bridge | 210000 | 22000 | 16 min | 17 min | 8 | Supported with separately funded dependencies |

All three expose actual quotes together on the customer and room inspector. The browser option uses cloud media, permits actual foreign participation, and provides no recording, captions or event entitlement. A paid recorder does not change that limitation. The authored Enterprise guest session lasts four minutes with two participants; its successful unrecorded completion cannot satisfy the recorded-room mission requirement. Mission evidence requires the actual session capture record and booking lineage.

Native replacement reapplies the native mode and eight-participant profile; product bridge commissioning restores eight participants. Quotes disclose existing room commitments. Explicit service retirement disables the old room on the next tick, retains the agreed minimum supplier exposure, and allows a fresh funded replacement. Replacing a profile does not erase prior bills or rewrite historical sessions.

Each new scheduled session retains its join method and room/profile generations. Validation checks the actual configuration for that generation and Provider configuration timing, including retirement. Older supported save schemas can retain sessions without these new fields; new Provider content requires the funded admission proof. The fields never grant media, capture or product entitlement.

`tests/provider-room-guest-choice.test.js` covers all three quoted versus actual delivery times, real browser participation and recording refusal, paid retirement/recovery, commitments, save/worker continuation, and forged or removed admission proof. `scripts/provider-room-guest-browser.js` exercises desktop and mobile controls; execution receipts remain outside Git and must name the tested candidate.
