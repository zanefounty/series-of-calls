# Recorded partner decision carried into the next meeting

The 13 November 2026 gameplay audit R13-10 asks for a later task that needs an actual earlier record or delivery outcome and retains the resulting promise. This bounded Enterprise path uses the synthetic partner meeting already required by the Canopy Group brief. It does not interpret a recording's contents or claim that every transcript has a campaign use.

Fresh visible Provider careers set `meetingContinuityVersion: 1`. Legacy saves without the marker keep their prior meeting schedule. A completed Partner Jules meeting must have a real capture record linked to its customer child session. Once such a record exists, the next period's partner meeting requires a player-selected schedule carrying that specific record. The inspector identifies the record, the form asks the player to accept the carry-forward promise, and the revision-bound quote repeats the commitment. The accepted plan retains the record ID, session ID and `carry-prior-partner-decision` promise in the Provider save. The chosen time still changes the actual booking.

At the next booking minute, the scheduler checks the retained plan and the recording's availability. Without the promise it records `PRIOR_MEETING_DECISION_REQUIRED`; if the record was removed it records `PRIOR_MEETING_RECORD_UNAVAILABLE`. Either outcome blocks that meeting's recorded-output credit. A valid promise admits the booking but does not manufacture a successful room: the paid room, guest admission, capture capacity and any active incident still decide whether the meeting completes. An actual completed recording appears in that period's customer evidence and can affect qualification and service credit. The previously accepted recording and promise remain in the save across later periods.

AC-SOC-903 covers a real first recorded meeting, a missing and wrong-record quote, stale revision refusal, a blocked later booking without a promise, a paid room incident repair, a completed later recorded meeting, distinct settled evidence, pure inspector and quote rendering, save/reload, Worker parity and forged promise rejection. AC-SOC-902 separately covers reactions for later named customers from their own work and bills. The new UI labels are registered in every locale with English fallback pending translation review.

This is a partial R13-10 implementation. Uncoached browser acceptance, later-sector authored relationships, transcript-specific tasks, and a promise with commercial consequences beyond one meeting remain open. The six-role career work and release gates remain separate.
