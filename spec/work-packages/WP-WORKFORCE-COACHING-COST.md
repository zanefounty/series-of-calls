# Workforce coaching cost reconciliation

`BEH-SOC-381`, acceptance cases `AC-SOC-580`–`592` plus the combined quality journey `AC-SOC-574`, is partial. Source IDs: `uc-new:domain:workforce`, `uc-new:domain:pricing`, `uc-new:domain:commercial-roles`.

An explicit validated `workforce/coach` command creates one timed job and its typed workforce cost event. With the business pack active it also posts one AUD-cent commercial cost row and a receipt on that job. The receipt identifies the person, skill, start time and exact ledger entry. It is separate from optional quality-review charges, workforce subscriptions and transport invoices. There is no revenue or supplier invoice for coaching.

The current people ownership/delegation and BPO action/expiry gates both apply before acceptance. A missing scoped grant fails atomically without a job, event or cost. A new current grant allows explicit retry. Accepted cost is immediate; the skill takes effect only after the declared duration. Subsequent grant expiry does not erase the accepted job or create another charge. A zero-cost command has an explicit zero receipt and no ledger row. Standalone workforce remains a valid alternative; historical jobs lacking business receipts are not retroactively billed when accounting starts.

Save validation binds receipts to jobs, retained cost events and commercial ledger rows. A first-commercial-job boundary distinguishes new billed jobs, including explicit zero cost, from older standalone history; removing a receipt together with its recent ledger row cannot reclassify the job as legacy. Exact start/due/completion timing, typed amounts and IDs are checked. Actual bounded event and ledger pruning retains historical proof; a missing recent event or row is rejected. Worker dispatch, pure inspection and deterministic request replay retain the same receipt. The declared coaching maximum is 10,000,000 AUD cents; it does not change the smaller external invoice cap.

The public recovery fixture traverses the same BPO failure, explicit grant, cost acceptance and delayed completion in AU, US, JP and EU synthetic boundary profiles. Its 125-cent price is a synthetic test value. This is not real training procurement, provider pricing, staffing certification or a full BPO agreement model. Owner review of proposed fictional aliases and full source dispositions remain outstanding.

Active coaching removes the person from eligible staffing and changes the workforce evidence fingerprint. Previously passed campaign windows remain historical but become stale for current assessment. Timed completion does not rewrite those observations; fresh baseline/service windows prove the changed configuration. Saves without active coaching retain the historical fingerprint shape.
