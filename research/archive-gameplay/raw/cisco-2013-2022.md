# Cisco VoIP archive incident/use-case research (2013–2022)

Research status: partial source set. Each item links directly to an individual archived message. “Cause” is classified from the post itself; suggestions remain hypotheses.

1. **Date:** 2013-02-15. **Title:** CUCM calls to selected PSTN prefixes have no audio. **Evidence:** CUCM-registered phone calls to specific prefixes/toll-free numbers connect without either party hearing audio; same destinations work from gateway FXS and CME phones. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare RTP paths and SDP across caller types; decide whether CUCM media anchoring differs. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2013-February/031324.html
2. **Date:** 2013-02-28. **Title:** CCD-over-SAF inbound calls stall in dead air. **Evidence:** Calls from CUCM clusters to remote CME phones produce no progress tones and no ring, while reverse direction works; responders ask whether call is incomplete versus connected without audio and recommend DNA. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Trace digit analysis and SAF route match, choose between call-routing and media paths. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2013-February/031456.html
3. **Date:** 2013-09-28. **Title:** Outbound DTMF is not relayed through CUBE. **Evidence:** Inbound provider-to-phone DTMF works, but phone-to-provider DTMF does not on an outbound CUBE SIP call. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare negotiated DTMF relay modes and codec path by direction. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2013-September/033441.html
4. **Date:** 2013-11-05. **Title:** Phone configuration parses incorrectly despite downloading. **Evidence:** 7945 receives and authenticates signed config, then XML parsing fails; other models also report verify/config issues. Suggestions include wrong device type, outdated load, or corrupted XML. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Validate TFTP payload, phone model provisioning, and firmware/time-zone compatibility before changing trust lists. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2013-November/033901.html
5. **Date:** 2013-11-30. **Title:** Mixed SIP/SCCP endpoints fail hold/resume. **Evidence:** A CUCM 8.6 remote site mixes SCCP with third-party SIP phones; thread concerns hold/resume behavior. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Reproduce hold/resume by protocol pair and inspect re-INVITE/REFER signaling. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2013-November/034197.html
6. **Date:** 2014-01-06. **Title:** Ringing continues after caller abandons inbound call. **Evidence:** Caller disconnects while destination phone is ringing; phone keeps ringing and answering produces silence. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Follow CANCEL/BYE handling across provider, CUBE, and CUCM; identify which leg fails to clear. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2014-January/034372.html
7. **Date:** 2014-04-30. **Title:** Blind transfer to PSTN creates one-way audio. **Evidence:** Phone-to-phone call blindly transferred through SIP trunk/CUBE to mobile; remote party hears audio but transferring IP phone does not, phone reports stream not ready. Suggested SIP SDP inspection; no confirmed root cause in this message. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Inspect mid-call SDP and test early-offer/SIP-profile behavior. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2014-April/035733.html
8. **Date:** 2014-06-05. **Title:** Intermittent phone resets drop active calls. **Evidence:** Phones unregister with connectivity error, TCP timeouts and fallback to another CUCM node; seen during network upgrades. **Cause status:** proposed (network suspected, not proven). **Playable investigation:** Correlate keepalive loss, switch changes, and cluster-node transitions. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2014-June/036128.html
9. **Date:** 2015-11-17. **Title:** CUCM refresh upgrade stalls at publisher. **Evidence:** Upgrade process times out at publisher despite services appearing up; a reply identifies NTP FQHN beginning with a digit as a known upgrade failure trigger, while another reports CPCD reboot helped a prior stage. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Examine upgrade stage, NTP configured hostname versus resolved status, and publisher reachability. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2015-November/041211.html
10. **Date:** 2016-02-08. **Title:** Music on hold disappears across CUBE. **Evidence:** Basic SIP calls work; on hold, CUCM emits MOH packets but they do not pass CUBE to provider, and audio returns after unhold. **Cause status:** proposed (media port/stream translation investigated). **Playable investigation:** Compare packet capture and SDP port negotiation across CUBE; decide whether duplex streaming workaround is appropriate. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-February/041685.html
11. **Date:** 2016-02-26. **Title:** VCS route returns 503 before reaching VCS. **Evidence:** CUCM logs 503 for calls routed to VCS; poster later finds the configured port changed and says this explained why calls did not reach VCS, though the overall issue remained partly unresolved. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Verify route-pattern target port and confirm arrival in VCS search history. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-February/041842.html
12. **Date:** 2016-05-04. **Title:** Authenticated ITSP registration uses private HA address. **Evidence:** ITSP reports CUBE REGISTER requests sourced from inside VIP rather than public interface; existing unauthenticated trunks continue working. **Cause status:** proposed (global binding/config scope discussed). **Playable investigation:** Trace registration source interface and compare global versus tenant-specific bind behavior. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-May/042220.html
13. **Date:** 2016-07-01. **Title:** Call recorder receives external mask instead of extension. **Evidence:** CUCM SIP INVITEs to a built-in-bridge recorder include external phone number mask on PSTN calls, while recorder expects four-digit extension. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Inspect trunk classification and calling-party transformation context; choose which identity recorder should consume. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-July/042593.html
14. **Date:** 2016-11-14. **Title:** UCCX HA upgrade degrades into fast busy. **Evidence:** After UCCX 10.5-to-11.5 upgrade, calls initially work then both HA nodes begin returning fast busy; failover restores service only briefly; rollback works, roll-forward reproduces after four hours. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare license, Live Data/ECDSA prerequisites, and time-to-failure telemetry before choosing rollback or further upgrade. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-November/043324.html
15. **Date:** 2016-11-28. **Title:** Inbound caller ID displays “Unknown Caller ID.” **Evidence:** New SIP trunk omits quoted calling number in From field; older trunk included it, and 6921 phones show unknown label with number hidden on the single-line display. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Compare inbound From/identity headers and determine whether to normalize presentation or preserve privacy behavior. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-November/043351.html
16. **Date:** 2016-12-22. **Title:** Phone call-history browsing sends presence SUBSCRIBE to PSTN trunk. **Evidence:** Opening placed-calls list triggers CUCM presence subscriptions for numbers routed through CUBE; blocking presence in both directions on the trunk stopped the messages. **Cause status:** confirmed by poster’s reported result. **Playable investigation:** Reproduce with BLF call lists, then scope a presence group policy to the carrier trunk. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2016-December/043472.html
17. **Date:** 2017-09-05. **Title:** CUBE binding change blocked by lingering call legs. **Evidence:** Adding a new dial-peer/control bind fails while active SIP legs remain; forced shutdown leaves some legs until SIP call service is stopped. **Cause status:** confirmed (active legs prevent bind change, per observed sequence). **Playable investigation:** Decide between a drain window and disruptive call clearing; inspect residual legs before reapplying bind. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2017-September/044893.html
18. **Date:** 2018-09-17. **Title:** Endpoint-local changes revert or fail to sync with CUCM. **Evidence:** CE endpoint settings appear to revert after reset; thread explains that CUCM provisioning can overwrite endpoint values, while supported local changes should synchronize back when provisioned and registered. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Check provisioning state and registration, then compare endpoint and CUCM configuration ownership. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2018-September/046676.html
19. **Date:** 2019-01-17. **Title:** CUCM/CUC/UCCX upgrade risk from replication drift. **Evidence:** In upgrade planning, a CUC subscriber reported healthy replication from publisher but failed when checked locally; historical hostname change left subscriber tables stale and required rebuild. **Cause status:** confirmed (reported historical cause). **Playable investigation:** Choose go/no-go based on replication checks performed independently on publisher and subscriber. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2019-January/047279.html
20. **Date:** 2019-07-26. **Title:** Forwarded caller reaches the wrong voicemail greeting. **Evidence:** PSTN caller reaches Diane, who forwards all calls to Bobbie; question is whether caller can hear Bobbie’s greeting. Discussion identifies global “Last Redirecting Number” behavior and concern over caller experience. **Cause status:** confirmed (configuration behavior reported working globally). **Playable investigation:** Decide voicemail mailbox selection policy and test redirecting-number handling. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2019-July/047932.html
21. **Date:** 2019-08-01. **Title:** Hunt pilot alerting name missing on phones. **Evidence:** 7800/8800 phones fail to display hunt pilot alerting name after CUCM 11.5(1)SU5+ changed Call-Info formatting; post attributes it to phone firmware defect and shares normalization workaround. **Cause status:** confirmed (defect cited in post). **Playable investigation:** Compare firmware and Call-Info header before changing SIP normalization. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/attachments/20190801/c3521011/attachment-0001.htm
22. **Date:** 2019-10-13. **Title:** UCCX IVR fails after migration from ISDN to SIP. **Evidence:** Inbound IVR calls get 404 although outbound works and dial-peer matching appears correct; reply proposes CSS/partition or trunk device-pool context. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Trace called number through ingress trunk CSS, partitions, device pool, and routing context. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2019-October/048395.html
23. **Date:** 2020-04-30. **Title:** CUCM rejects inbound SIP call with 404. **Evidence:** CUCM 12.0 receives INVITE but responds 404; outgoing works and caller has checked CSS and direct phone targeting. Reply raises MTP allocation/DTMF and trunk configuration questions, not a confirmed diagnosis. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Inspect inbound called-number normalization, CSS access, MTP allocation and dial-peer signaling. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2020-April/049416.html
24. **Date:** 2020-10-14. **Title:** 7945/7965 firmware upgrade authentication loop. **Evidence:** After CUCM 10.5(2)SU10, replacement phones repeatedly fail load authentication for a firmware image; installing intermediate and then target COP loads succeeded. Poster suspects bad bundled firmware copy. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Separate trust-list diagnosis from image integrity and test staged firmware installation. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2020-October/049918.html
25. **Date:** 2020-12-22. **Title:** UCCX upgrade rejects CUCM AXL authentication. **Evidence:** During UCCX 10.6-to-12.5 build, Service Provider setup returns CCMAXL authentication exception despite existing AXL user/roles; incorrect password produces a visible credential failure. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Check AXL API access, publisher/subscriber target, certificate trust, and logs before rebuilding. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2020-December/049937.html
26. **Date:** 2021-02-18. **Title:** CUCM bulk device reports truncate on large clusters. **Evidence:** After CUCM 12.5 SU3, export and UDP/phone reports fail around 700–800 devices in clusters with thousands of users and tens of thousands of endpoints; TAC identified two bugs, further rollouts were held. **Cause status:** confirmed (TAC identified defects; IDs not given in excerpt). **Playable investigation:** Test report size boundaries and weigh API/CLI extraction against upgrade rollout. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/attachments/20210218/fad5abe6/attachment.htm
27. **Date:** 2021-09-29. **Title:** CUCM reports near-full active partition after migration. **Evidence:** Fresh PCD migration install shows active partition at 98%; reports suggest standard partition threshold may be noisy, while older OVA sizing can make later upgrade/redeployment difficult. **Cause status:** proposed. **Playable investigation:** Compare disk usage and OVA sizing; decide whether alert threshold or capacity migration is warranted. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2021-September/050292.html
28. **Date:** 2022-02-03. **Title:** External voicemail messages cut off at 49 seconds. **Evidence:** Unity 12.5 SU4 messages from external callers truncate at 49 seconds despite a 300-second limit; internal voicemail calls work, and two sites report it. **Cause status:** unknown. **Playable investigation:** Compare SIP/RTP and gateway timers for external versus internal message sessions. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/cisco-voip/2022-February/050485.html
