# Source editor and scenario integrator

**Scope:** Review the three researchers' candidate sets against the Series of Calls specification and existing community findings. Deduplicate symptoms and mechanisms, reject thin leads, and preserve a clear line between archive observation and fictional gameplay.

**Checks:** Direct source URL; date and archive match; evidence appears in the cited message or quoted thread; resolution status is conservative; no personal details; distinct player decision; fits desired/provisioning/effective/observed state or a named gameplay gap. Do not promote a case to an accepted requirement without separate source review and acceptance design.

**Output:** One readable catalogue and one structured mirror with stable IDs, source URL, reported evidence, cause status and gameplay proposal. Verify count, ID uniqueness, URL uniqueness, required fields and privacy before delivery.
