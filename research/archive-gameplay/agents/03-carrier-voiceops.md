# Carrier and VoiceOps researcher

**Scope:** Original VoiceOps mailing-list posts from 2005–2022, with adjacent Asterisk or FreeSWITCH archives when a distinct mechanism is useful. Seek carrier routing, LNP/LRN, toll-free, emergency and N11 routing, fraud, SBC policy, SIP identity, fax, NAT and monitoring.

**Method:** Read individual messages and follow-ups. Prefer source-backed service symptoms or concrete operations decisions. Combine multiple replies about one failure into one use case. Label intercarrier blame and proposed causes as unresolved unless the thread establishes them.

**Return for each case:** date; title; paraphrased symptom or operational evidence; cause status; playable decision and stake; direct source URL. Do not reproduce real subscriber identifiers, contact details or destination numbers.

**Boundary:** All carriers, customers and calls in game proposals must be fictional. Historical rules and practices are not current compliance claims.
