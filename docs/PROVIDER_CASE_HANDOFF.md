# Same-case channel handoff

This is an unreleased `provider-campaigns-8` behavior with the optional `caseHandoffVersion: 1` marker. Published campaigns without that marker retain their previous demand plan. Unknown versions and use of the marker on an earlier content version are rejected.

SMB customers with a paid, effective Cisco programmable engagement service still receive 24 cases per full period. Of the four authored optional social-message preferences, three start digitally and one starts as a voice call whose customer permits a later digital handoff. The other twelve voice obligations remain voice. The optional waiting call can also complete normally; accepting the handoff is a decision, not a requirement for service credit. Other missions keep their authored protected demand.

The selected customer's journey offers a quote for an admitted, queued, unassigned voice case. It shows the original deadline, voice usage and the customer's actual plan rates. The choice has no additional setup charge; the already purchased product and all existing commitments remain payable. A licensed, available customer handler and current paid channel capability are required. Mandatory voice, emergency, recorded/protected, assigned, expired, settled and previously handed-off work cannot use this action.

`SOC.provider.quote` supplies terms and `SOC.dispatch` owns the atomic transaction, revision check and request idempotency. The original voice interaction ends with `CHANNEL_HANDOFF`, which is not a delivered resolution. A real digital interaction starts with the same tenant, person and case. Both traces record reciprocal lineage. The same job keeps its deadline and authored obligation, meters its actual voice usage and charges one accepted message. Only the digital leg's actual human resolution completes that job. Period close and deadline expiry stop pending handoff work and release its reservations.

A typed plan anchor is checked against the authored scenario, seed, period, arrival schedule, canonical case IDs and paid historical channel capability. The handoff receipt must agree with that plan and the actual paid project, both interactions and their case record. Derived pins keep both legs while their parent job is retained. Normal job pruning removes those pins; Lab export removes Provider ownership markers. Rendering and journey explanations do not meter or complete work.

Verification is recorded separately. The helper/planner/UI counterexamples are AC-SOC-657–672; public paid dispatch, once-only settlement, stale/duplicate commands and restore/worker continuation are AC-SOC-686–687. These IDs are not a claim that a final frozen candidate or browser matrix has passed. This queued-voice transition also does **not** prove the separate L8 requirement for a human-resolved voice interaction followed by human-resolved digital work for the same case.
