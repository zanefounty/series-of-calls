# Domain, ownership and ecosystem

An organisation has an identity and one or more commercial roles. A product is a capability family owned by an organisation. An offer packages a product, edition, charging rules and sale conditions. A deployment is an instance of a product at a version, tenancy boundary and location. An integration is a scoped directional relationship between deployments. A contract identifies the buyer, seller, service operator, support owner, obligations and exit terms. These objects have independent lifecycles.

The catalogue appendix retains every mapped company, customer template, location, asset, supply relationship, merger, commercial role and source parameter. A mapping row is not necessarily a distinct company or physical object. Two rows may refer to the same fictional representation while specifying different obligations. Such rows may share tested mechanics only when each distinctive parameter and dependency is represented and tested.

## Role and authority model

Vendors own products and define supported capabilities. Distributors aggregate purchasing and fulfilment. Resellers sell offers without necessarily operating deployments. Integrators implement change projects. MSPs operate delegated services and own contracted support responsibilities. Carriers provide numbers, interconnect and calling services. Wholesalers sell underlying capacity or service packages. CPaaS suppliers expose synthetic channel APIs; builders own workflows and customer applications. BPOs operate contracted human workforces. Customers own business requirements and may operate their own agents.

Ownership never implies every administrative permission. Delegation records scope, allowed operations, granting principal, expiry and support escalation. Qualifications apply to a supplier, offer and market with versioned validity. A distributor relationship does not transfer product ownership; an acquisition changes ownership while preserving historical contract parties and artefact custody. A reseller cannot edit a customer-controlled workforce merely because it supplies the contact-centre platform.

## Identity and deployment boundaries

Users have organisation membership, directory identity, entitlements, dialling permissions, endpoints and location scopes. An endpoint registration references a user or shared-service identity. Room accounts are service identities with calendar and device permissions. Agent identities additionally need skill, language, shift and availability records. Paid entitlement, device registration and available human time are independent attributes.

Multitenant UC, dedicated hosted UC, premises UC, partner-operated private service and explicitly disconnected service are distinct deployments. Reuse of a vendor-style console does not merge their prerequisites. Dedicated services can retain legacy application dependencies absent from a multitenant product. Local appliances can still require external licensing or administration.

Compatibility edges specify source and target profiles, direction, feature subset, product versions, cloud/region, authentication, licences, operator and support owner. Evidence labels distinguish officially documented support, certification, marketplace listing, vendor claim, scenario test and unknown. A marketplace listing alone never establishes government eligibility or all-feature interoperability.

## Fictionalisation

Source vendor names remain in research; gameplay identities are fictional. Existing fictional names such as Level Tree can remain. Proposed rows labelled as real-vendor reference profiles require a naming decision and a capability review before becoming playable. A simple renamed copy is insufficient when deployment or channel models differ. Fictional quality, prices and satire cannot be attributed to a real vendor.

The foundation implements Microsnoft Workplace Phone, Disco Dedicated Voice, Level Tree Access, Bellwether Interconnect, Disco Service Desk, Chatterleaf Business, Disco Connective, Casebook, Echo Vault, People Orchard and Clockwork Local Services. They are deliberately limited archetypes, not claims of real product parity. The full 3,454-row catalogue remains available for subsequent implementation and review.

## Resource dimensions

The schemas must preserve Mbps/Gbit/s for transport; seats for named entitlements; sessions for concurrent call/media resources; agents for workforce or concurrent-agent entitlements with the exact subtype recorded; minutes for time; messages for channel events; rooms for room entitlements; GB for storage; and processor-minutes for processing work. Currency uses integer minor units and explicit currency. No arithmetic may turn bandwidth into paid seats or staffed people. Conversion from a workload into required bandwidth needs an explicit codec/media profile, concurrency and overhead model.
