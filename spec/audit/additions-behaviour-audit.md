The detailed rows below preserve the 3 November audit. Their consoles and root/worker/save paths are integrated. See [customer needs](../work-packages/WP-CUSTOMER-NEEDS.md), [workforce adherence](../work-packages/WP-WORKFORCE-ADHERENCE.md), and [webhooks](../work-packages/WP-CHANNEL-WEBHOOKS.md).

These changes do not close the segment/domain rows. Subsequent customer-fit work measures configured requirements against observed service, but full procurement and commercial consequences remain open. `BEH-SOC-378` adds one proposed fictional Queueberry WFM offer with tenant/version-scoped integration, named forecast coverage and prepaid costs; see [its bounded contract](../work-packages/WP-WORKFORCE-OFFER.md). This supersedes the historical statement that every actor has no implementation, while leaving broader actor portfolios, complete workforce/BPO planning and source-specific CPaaS APIs open. Exact source-ID presence proves a link, not semantic completion. The detailed 3 November rows below remain a historical baseline.
