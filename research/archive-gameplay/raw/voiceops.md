# VoiceOps archive gameplay use cases (other)

A curated set of distinct historical communications operations cases and decisions, based on individual VoiceOps archive messages. Cause status reflects what the cited post establishes. No personal contact details are reproduced.

1. **Date:** 2016-10-01. **Title:** T.38 renegotiation yields malformed UDPTL. **Evidence:** Grandstream GXW-4248 fax audio cuts at T.38 renegotiation; re-INVITE appears successful, first UDPTL packet is flagged malformed. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Compare IFP payload and negotiation with working ATA; isolate firmware vs interop. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2016-October/006847.html
2. **Date:** 2010-12-15. **Title:** Dynamic T1 fax works for some customers only. **Evidence:** Adtran TA900/Cisco IAD2400 faxing is inconsistent despite T.38 preference, G.711 fallback, QoS, clean circuits, and no observed loss/jitter. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Build controlled tests across gateway, fax speed, and analog path. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2010-December/002108.html
3. **Date:** 2011-02-08. **Title:** H.323 compromise triggers satellite toll calls. **Evidence:** A related customer system reportedly placed concurrent expensive satellite calls, yielding a large bill. **Cause:** Confirmed unauthorized calling; precise exploit path unknown. **Play:** Contain access, preserve CDRs, identify exposed ingress/authentication. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2011-February/002293.html
4. **Date:** 2012-09-04. **Title:** Known-working number fails on another carrier. **Evidence:** Carrier asks how to resolve subscribers unable to dial a known-working number on another network. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Define ownership of trace and collect origin, destination, route, translation evidence. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2012-September/003540.html
5. **Date:** 2020-03-26. **Title:** No simple nationwide N11 routing map. **Evidence:** Carrier describes maintaining its own geography-sensitive 211/311/511/711/811 translations and asks for machine-readable data. **Cause:** Confirmed operational data gap. **Play:** Choose routing key, exception handling, and validation source. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2020-March/008394.html
6. **Date:** 2011-06. **Title:** SBC blocks registrations with IP Request-URI. **Evidence:** Post describes SBC rejecting registrations when Request-URI uses an IP address and suggests a dummy-agent pattern. **Cause:** Confirmed behavior described. **Play:** Normalize, sink, or reject such requests deliberately. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2011-June/002582.html
7. **Date:** 2011-07-19. **Title:** Follow-me call loses audio after acceptance. **Evidence:** Allworx forwards an inbound call back out; after mobile presses 1, audio disappears both ways although RTP is seen on multiple legs. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Trace SDP/RTP tuples across re-INVITE and find first unusable leg. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2011-July/002640.html
8. **Date:** 2010-11-09. **Title:** Ported calls arrive at carrier's own LRN. **Evidence:** Some LNP calls arrive without GAP and route to company PBX because main number equals LRN. **Cause:** Proposed upstream GAP stripping. **Play:** Compare successful/failed ISUP IAMs; route LRN to monitored sink while tracing. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2010-November/002027.html
9. **Date:** 2015-10-13. **Title:** Invalid numbers return 503 and are retried. **Evidence:** Call-center dialer treats 503 as transient, repeatedly redialing invalid numbers; carriers map causes as 503/404/604/CANCEL/487. **Cause:** Confirmed ambiguity and retry behavior. **Play:** Set retry classes without suppressing valid calls. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2015-October/005925.html
10. **Date:** 2014-05-06. **Title:** Canadian-origin calls to toll-free numbers blocked. **Evidence:** Canadian DIDs fail to reach some toll-free numbers via several US SIP carriers; US origins work. **Cause:** Proposed origin/LATA or provider policy. **Play:** Compare ANI, geography, path, and toll-free translation. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2014-May/004720.html
11. **Date:** 2015-06-22. **Title:** Intermittent silent calls to mobile network. **Evidence:** Calls to Rogers connect but lack far-end audio across wholesale carriers/interconnects; SS7 traces show little inbound media after ringing. **Cause:** Proposed — destination network suspected, not confirmed. **Play:** Correlate by destination, route, time; escalate with traces. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2015-June/005645.html
12. **Date:** 2015-06-20. **Title:** Select a geographically redundant class-4 switch. **Evidence:** Provider planning direct interconnect seeks active-active geographic redundancy and support. **Cause:** Design decision, no incident. **Play:** Rank resilience/support/migration criteria and require failure test. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2015-June/005619.html
13. **Date:** 2015-12-17. **Title:** NATed endpoint becomes unreachable. **Evidence:** Post discusses TCP keepalive, TLS heartbeat, or SIP OPTIONS to maintain reachability; OPTIONS timeouts can tear down registration. **Cause:** Proposed. **Play:** Balance detection time against signaling load. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2015-December/006244.html
14. **Date:** 2013-01-17. **Title:** SPA1x2 locks up or stops registering. **Evidence:** Ports randomly show off-hook, reject calls, fail registration, or web portal hangs across firmware versions. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Gather firmware/hardware cohort and port telemetry before replacement. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2013-January/003861.html
15. **Date:** 2015-11. **Title:** Text-enabled number routing needs portability data. **Evidence:** Discussion notes accurate routing for voice and text and portability-corrected NNID identifiers. **Cause:** Proposed design risk. **Play:** Define inventory checks before porting text-enabled numbers. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2015-November/006074.html
16. **Date:** 2018-08-29. **Title:** LNP numbers may hit wrong tandem. **Evidence:** Discussion describes ported blocks that should route by LRN but may follow thousand-block/tandem homing. **Cause:** Proposed. **Play:** Determine whether failed calls track LRN or legacy rate-center route. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/attachments/20180829/5d30796e/attachment-0001.html

18. **Date:** 2022-06-28. **Title:** Verifier rejects locally signed PASSporTs. **Evidence:** Calls with customer Identity header fail validation while calls without it pass using carrier-generated PASSporT, across two carriers. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Compare TN, certificate chain, header preservation, and signing claims per hop. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/attachments/20220628/d235ffe6/attachment.htm
19. **Date:** 2022-07-03. **Title:** Identity headers disappear on toll-free paths. **Evidence:** Test tool reports limited success receiving Identity headers on toll-free numbers from multiple providers. **Cause:** Unknown. **Play:** Compare geographic DID and toll-free call paths to locate header loss. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2022-July/009633.html
20. **Date:** 2011-09-16. **Title:** Asterisk runs out of RTP ports under call-center load. **Evidence:** Asterisk acting as SIP/media proxy for about 30 agents is reported unable to allocate RTP ports; config shows a bounded RTP range. **Cause:** Proposed (port capacity is a lead, not proven). **Play:** Compare concurrent calls/conferences to allocated ports and decide whether to expand range or capacity. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2011-September/002775.html
21. **Date:** 2010-06-09. **Title:** SIP reachability checks miss failed call completion. **Evidence:** Operator asks for automated end-to-end tests beyond ping/SIP gateway availability, covering legacy numbers, multiple routes, and actual media. **Cause:** Confirmed monitoring gap. **Play:** Design synthetic calls that verify both signaling and recorded tones/media. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2010-June/001579.html
22. **Date:** 2019-05-14. **Title:** IVR fails when caller sends in-band DTMF. **Evidence:** School IVR accepts RFC2833 only; some callers support in-band DTMF and the SBC/softswitch path cannot transcode. **Cause:** Confirmed codec/interworking gap. **Play:** Choose a DSP-capable SBC or adjust endpoint/media path and test digit recognition. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2019-May/008033.html

25. **Date:** 2012-07. **Title:** Caller ID differs between From and P-Asserted-Identity. **Evidence:** Operators discuss which SIP identity header governs caller ID and interconnect behavior. **Cause:** Unknown (configuration/interoperability question). **Play:** Trace header values across trusted boundaries and choose canonical identity handling. **Source:** https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2012-July/003435.html
