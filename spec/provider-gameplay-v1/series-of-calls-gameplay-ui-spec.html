1. Product decision

Make Series of Calls a provider management game by default. The player wins communications customers, builds and operates their services, manages constrained people and money, and decides where to expand. Use the upstream game frame and replace the network-business mechanics with UC/CC equivalents.

Keep the existing simulation, its useful training exercises and its detailed diagnostic tools. Put those tools behind the decisions that need them. The normal opening must be understandable as a game before it is understandable as an administrator console.

Deliverable status. This is a proposed implementation specification, not a claim that the rebuild exists. The companion prompt instructs an agent to implement it. The wireframe is illustrative. Prices, pacing and numerical test targets below are proposed game balancing values, not vendor quotes or research-derived guarantees.

3. What the game must make the player do

The repeated activity is choose a customer opportunity → compare feasible service plans → commit cash and staff → watch real work arrive → respond to demand or failure → collect and explain the bill → renew, improve or expand. Every major screen must help with one of those decisions.

Design lensApplication hereObservable proof
MDAStart with the desired experience of building, maintaining and growing a working service. Tune rules and interactions to produce it.A service investment changes later demand handling, risk and finances; adding modules alone is insufficient.
Meaningful playPlayers can see alternatives, understand an action's effect and see its significance later.A cheaper plan leaves cash for expansion but creates a visible exposure or limitation.
GameFlowGive clear goals, feedback and controllable challenge. Reveal complexity when the next decision requires it.An uncoached player can identify the current goal, the failure cause and a useful next action.
Autonomy and competenceOffer more than one feasible strategy and help players learn cause and effect.A player can explain why their plan worked, then adapt it to a changed constraint.

These are design lenses, not a numerical fun certificate. References: Hunicke, LeBlanc and Zubek, MDA; Zimmerman on meaningful choices; Sweetser/Kyburz on GameFlow; Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski on motivation.

Comparable design patterns are visible constrained routing in Mini Metro, a construction-to-income loop in OpenTTD, and useful automation after mastery in Factorio. Borrow the patterns selectively. This game does not need factory-scale resource chains or a geographic map for every dependency.

Interaction contract

  • Click a customer to inspect its current job, contract and next useful investment. Click a supplier to compare eligible offers. Click a path to see its supported service and constrained unit.
  • Drag from a customer/site to a compatible service or location to open a build/connection quote. The equivalent Connect/Expand button supports keyboard and touch users. Invalid endpoints explain why they are incompatible.
  • A purchase card shows cost now, cost /period, variable usage exposure, minimum term, lead time, engineering effort, capacity and feature limits. Show cash available after commitment and the estimated effect on margin.
  • Queued or provisioning work has a visible progress state. Solid active paths mean an effective service exists; activity and outcomes show whether it actually delivered work. Never animate a proposed purchase as already operational.
  • Use a focus/selection colour separately from healthy/warning/failure states. Pair colour with text and shape. The yellow action colour does not itself mean warning.
  • Retain focus, scroll, camera, selected scope and unfinished drafts through ticks. Use the existing stable update approach where suitable; do not replace the entire campaign DOM after every dispatch.
  • Keep primary body text around 14–16px, tabular money/capacity values, 44px primary touch targets and a visible keyboard focus. Respect reduced motion and optional sound. Avoid hover-only information.
  • Deep protocol traces, raw records and schema editors are optional diagnostics or Lab content. Ordinary players should see a clear cause such as "No eligible agents on shift" before an internal error code.

The desktop wireframe shows one relationship layer. At a wider portfolio scale, collapse each tenant to a summary card; expand it to inspect its services. Keep independent tenants distinct even when they share a supplier. This avoids turning a large installation into an unreadable line diagram.

Opening balance fixture

Use fictional AUD cents and author all values in one versioned scenario file. A starting candidate is A$20,000 capital and a starter brief worth A$1,800 per 60-minute period. Plan A might require A$6,000 upfront and A$1,350 total forecast operating costs per period, leaving A$450 contribution before expansion; Plan B might require A$10,000 upfront and A$1,050 forecast costs, leaving A$750 but less cash for the next deal. These are aggregate balance targets, not prices for real products or additional charges to stack on top of themselves. Decompose them into the actual offer, staff, carrier and integration ledger entries and prove the totals.

Give Plan B a clear operational or lead-time tradeoff as well as the higher capital requirement. A later change in channel demand, provider terms or delivery deadline must sometimes favour Plan A. If one plan dominates every tested case, revise the scenario. Do not manufacture a fake choice by making one card strictly worse.

The wireframe's optional A$240/period BPO response reduces a forecast A$450 margin to A$210. Callbacks have a setup/work cost and move eligible work into quieter time; they do not create agents or guarantee eventual completion. Customer-owned CC staff are not automatically MSP employees. The contract states who supplies and pays for them.

An accepted order can complete an ordering milestone while its service remains visibly pending. Dependent jobs may queue. As soon as the next goal requires delivery, highlight Resume. Do not strand the player in a paused provisioning step until coach step 4. Recognise valid steps completed in another order. Buying, commissioning and successful delivery are different events.

Executable mission contract

Every scenario must author demand/arrival windows, case deadlines, activation, service targets, credit schedule, renewal/exit, arrears/grace/default and a sustained victory window. The companion scenario-contract.example.json supplies proposed SMB defaults: 24 due cases per full period, including 16 voice and eight digital; at least 22 completed on time in each of two consecutive full periods; positive provider operating result in both; no overdue critical obligations; and a funded next contract. Partial or empty periods cannot count. Prove both reference plans can satisfy the rule under supported seeds, then record any tuning as a versioned scenario change.

The illustrative wait warning is two simulation minutes against a one-simulation-minute target. Do not display fabricated second-level precision when the canonical service model advances in integer minutes. Visual interpolation may be smooth; measured values retain their real resolution.

7. Rules that make the UI a game

Supply, commitment and work

Represent a player action at the level of an operation: deploy a tenant, connect a carrier, migrate a cohort, commission a room, schedule a quality review or repair a known fault. The operation may contain many validated configuration changes. Charge and schedule the operation once. Do not charge for every low-level field edit or make administrative clicking the scarce resource.

Quotes are pure. Committing a quote atomically checks cash/credit, ownership, capacity, compatible versions, delegated authority, staff and terms. It reserves funds and work slots, starts provisioning and records a receipt. A changed revision triggers recalculation and a readable explanation. Plans can be cancelled under authored refund and minimum-term rules.

One clock and declared units

Keep the current canonical integer simulation minute and the 60-minute commercial period. Initially, 1× advances one simulation minute every two real seconds. A billing period therefore takes about two real minutes when uninterrupted. Provide pause, 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4× and 8×. The HUD says Period 1, Minute 42/60; rates say /period. Do not label these prices monthly or treat the synthetic minute as a real operational forecast.

Decision modals pause consistently; closing one restores the previous speed unless the player explicitly changed it. Hidden tabs, lost editing ownership, storage/worker failures requiring intervention and terminal outcomes pause and show why. Cap accumulated frame time. No silent offline earnings or bankruptcy. High speed processes every relevant interval and interrupt, not just the last state.

Keep named seats, concurrent sessions, staffed agents, digital concurrency, room occupancy, message rate, processor-minutes, storage and transport Mbps separate. Queue waiting, connected carrier occupancy, talk time and billable customer usage can differ. Explain the unit relevant to each purchase and failure.

One stream of customer work

The scenario scheduler creates seeded customer jobs. Calls and messages take their real paths through the existing kernel. Admission, queueing, media, human handling, recording and processing produce outcomes. The canvas samples those events. It cannot generate extra paid interactions or calculate a separate success rate.

Use exact individual jobs for the opening and representative detailed traces. At scale, bounded cohorts and interval accumulators are allowed only if they preserve the same admission/outcome rules and accounting. An animated trace of a cohort is a view of its work, not extra work. Case continuity prevents one handoff from becoming two completed customer requests.

Shared money and shared people

The provider owns one treasury, debt agreement and implementation/support team. Each customer has a scoped service state and attributed ledger. Creating a tenant never creates capital. An investment, grant or loan needs its own explicit receipt. Consolidation eliminates internal transfers and preserves external revenues/costs.

Implementation engineers process deployments and changes; support staff process investigation and repair work. CC agents handle customer work under the contract's ownership model. Licences permit use, people provide labour, schedules determine availability, and skills/concurrency govern eligible work. Understaffing should cause a relevant queue, delay or lost opportunity. Healthy service need not instantly fail when an MSP support person is removed.

Show both current spendable cash and future exposure. Block unaffordable discretionary commitments unless a disclosed credit offer funds them. A posted bill can create arrears, then a visible grace/default/rescue sequence. It must not crash the simulation or allow indefinite unexplained negative cash.

Billing and renewal

Accumulate the actual history of offered, admitted, completed, failed, abandoned and unresolved work, eligible unavailable time, quality and required information outcomes. Contract terms define which facts determine retail charges, credits and renewal. Wholesale usage is a separate obligation; failed customer service can still cost the provider money.

A last-minute repair cannot erase a period of failure. A last-minute outage cannot erase service already delivered. Subscription revenue need not be zero in every bad period, but credits, price acceptance, churn, contract expiry and failure states must prevent an indefinitely profitable non-service strategy. Changing customer willingness to pay or forcing acceptance is scenario authorship, not player pricing.

Incidents and market pressure

Use authored, seeded incidents attached to real dependencies. Give a useful warning or diagnostic cue where the scenario promises one. Offer costed repair, temporary outsourcing, eligible rerouting, deferred work, use of previously purchased resilience or acceptance of loss. A one-click repair is acceptable when it starts real work with cost, duration and prerequisites.

WhatsApp/Messenger, Slack/workplace chat and video can shift eligible demand, replace a portion of paid usage, create integration opportunities or make a bundled suite more attractive. The customer's actual requirements bound substitution. Messaging cannot remove a mandatory PSTN or emergency-calling requirement. Supplier bundles and vendor-direct terms change competing offers; they are not player-operated market sliders.

Progression and replay

Unlock new customer classes, partner terms, deployment types, geography, staffing choices and automation. Use the existing goal/level structure but give each milestone a consequence for the next decision. Keep training badges and credits separate. New seeds vary demand shape, available supply, term pressure or incident exposure enough to change a useful plan. Difficulty changes disclosed starting resources or risk, not hidden success rules.

All four segment scenarios are directly available from Scenarios with authored starting assets and budgets, while career unlocks preserve a longer progression. Entering a scenario does not grant an earned career award. Do not lock a required tool behind the same assessment that needs it.

Save and authority model

Use a versioned experience discriminator such as campaign/training/lab. Existing guided/assessed/sandbox are learning modes, and existing SOC_CAMPAIGN is the training-track scheduler. Do not silently change either meaning. Store content identity/version, seed, difficulty and the fixed campaign rules at creation.

Campaign commands carry eligible intent IDs and quantities. The engine derives prices, capacity and success from authored content and state. A scenario-only method cannot be unlocked by sending a public system:true field. This is semantic integrity for an offline game, not a claim of cryptographic anti-cheat.

The present validator requires 15 canonical resource IDs. A safe first implementation may retain them as disabled, uncommissioned blueprints, with no available service, entitlement, capacity or income. A genuinely sparse model is also acceptable after auditing all fixed-ID lookups. Neither approach should expose 15 starting chores to the player.

Migrate legacy saves using their original validator before converting an owned copy. Keep old training progress as training. Do not invent historical period measurements. Preserve exportable originals and fail clearly on unknown schema/content versions. Keep campaign, training and SP storage/locks distinct, and retain monotonic save sequences and cross-tab ownership checks.

Put camera, selected customer, open drawer and unsubmitted drafts in view state. Merely inspecting a customer must not create a model command, consume a revision or reset its commercial clock.

12. Acceptance and critical review

Required visible playthrough

A fresh run must show a costed customer plan, purchased and commissioned service, autonomous demand, a consequential incident, a history-based bill, renewal or exit, and expansion using shared resources. Repeat with a different viable plan. Then complete one distinct mission for each of enterprise, government and defence. Use the actual candidate UI, not only direct dispatch scripts.

Reject the rebuild if any of these remain

  • The default opening is still a resource editor, a setup wizard, or a long page of renamed configuration cards.
  • The map is decorative, the buttons use mock outcomes, or the visible money is disconnected from the authoritative commercial ledger.
  • The campaign player can inject world events, type supplier prices, declare success or edit customer requirements.
  • A last-minute health snapshot determines the entire bill, or a customer can mint new opening capital.
  • Every mission is the same fixed repair checklist with different labels, or every reasonable plan collapses to the same dominant purchase.
  • Badges substitute for new choices, repairs are unconditional state toggles, or staff are only a cost with no operational effect.
  • Desktop and mobile screenshots look polished but no recorded UI journey reaches delivery, settlement and expansion.
  • Catalogue rows, test counts or an SMB demo are reported as proof of the full conversion.

Automated and browser checks

The companion requirements.json defines 52 proposed requirements with concrete acceptance cases and implementation owners. Tests should assert state, money, capacity, outcomes and visible explanations. Reuse the existing verification ladder in docs/DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md. Run focused suites while developing, then the required integration, evidence, source/coverage, gate mutation, translation and build checks on the candidate. Full release gates remain distinct from a playable milestone.

Browser evidence must state the build commit, actual served URL, browser/version, viewport, seed, steps, expected result, actual result and console errors. Check 1366×768 and 1440×900 desktop layouts and a 390×844 mobile layout, keyboard-only play, reduced motion, save/reload, two-tab ownership, offline reload after successful installation and the deployment prefix. A DOM assertion is not browser evidence. If browser access is blocked, report that check as pending.

Test stable drafts while time advances. Run equal simulation-time commands at 1× and 8×. Save during active calls, queued jobs, recording, coaching, a deployment and an incident. Re-import and compare the next outcome. Include the 2,001-minute regression and a declared 20-customer, 10,000-minute headless fixture; measure a representative UI load on stated hardware rather than inventing a universal frame-rate claim.

Exploratory human pilot

Prepare 6–8 participants split between UC practitioners and management-game players. Counterbalance upstream-first and port-first order. Let people play without verbal coaching, then ask them to explain the objective, a tradeoff, why a customer succeeded or failed, and what they would buy next. Change a constraint or seed and observe whether they revise the plan. Offer optional continued play.

Record time to first consequential choice, time to visible service, setup/inspection time, meaningful decisions, interventions, cause-and-effect explanations and voluntary continuation. A proposed first target is six of eight participants explaining the goal and one tradeoff without assistance, with nobody trapped by a missing essential control. Small samples guide iteration; they do not establish a scientific fun score. Mark the pilot pending until real people take part. The implementation agent can prepare the study, not impersonate its participants.

The 20-customer long-play fixture must contain 20 active customers with declared nonzero sustained workload, recurring settlement and at least one change/incident. Inactive or empty roots do not qualify. Compare deterministic continuation and accounting after pruning and save/restore.
