## Concrete source mechanics

### NBN POI access

The fork has a bounded wholesale-access mechanic. A POP at an NBN POI has no broadband throughput until an NNI port is ordered. Port orders incur setup and monthly charges, take a deterministic number of game days to become effective, and impose a hard Gbps ceiling. An upgrade leaves the prior port capacity in effect while the replacement is provisioned. Broadband subscribers are bounded from the port's busy-hour capacity and each active service contributes wholesale AVC charges. Hubs hand off in the provider's own DC and do not require a POI NNI. Save migration validates port size/readiness, drops invalid/non-owned POI state, and decommissioning removes the port with the POP.

Source: [NBN constants and cost contract](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/10-catalog/10-economy.js), [POI model](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/20-world/45-pois.js), [NNI commands and effective capacity](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/90-actions/25-nbn.js), [five NBN behavior tests](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/tests/nbn-poi.test.js).

The tests establish both success and meaningful failures: no port means launch is refused with no charge; a port's order/ready/effective dates are checked; a 10G NNI stops area growth until a 100G upgrade takes effect; all home AVCs reconcile to wholesale revenue; malformed or out-of-scope saves are cleaned; and the panel is checked at phone width in six languages.

### Data-center rack hosting

Australian data centers can lease a 10-rack cage (14-day fit-out) or build an 80-rack hall (120-day fit-out, only in a sufficiently large city). The monthly lease begins on order. Colocation and dedicated-server offers reserve rack capacity when accepted, draw down free capacity, and earn per-rack recurring revenue; dedicated servers also have a purchase charge. Oversized offers are rejected without a charge, and space cannot be returned while occupied. POIs and overseas sites cannot host this business; saves clamp rack use to supported quantities and remove DC-space state from invalid locations.

Source: [hosting rules/operations](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/60-features/45-hosting.js), [hosted business tiers](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/10-catalog/70-hosting.js), [four hosting tests](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/tests/hosting.test.js).

### Physical transport, peering, and incident/recovery behavior

- The revised geography distinguishes a cable's landing stations from through waypoints. Cable segments terminate at landings; a player needs a POP at an intermediate landing to continue onward. Landing stations do not sell transit, exchange ports, peering, cache, data-center space, or customer service. POI, data-center, and overseas-peering roles have explicit eligibility rules; the game tests both action refusal and invalid-state cleanup on load. Source: [world-map role and landing tests](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/tests/world-map.test.js), [map and cable data](https://github.com/thesysadmindev/SeriesOfTubesAU/blob/c650d84d0f79219e8b2249bae9bac3bbfe3b2d5e/src/game/20-world/60-cables.js).
- The AU branch's main transport changes are mostly route/cable graph and location rebalance to fit its geography. Many selected sea-routing, optic, shared-failure, peering, and recovery behaviors in `tests/sea-routing.test.js`, `tests/issues.test.js`, and `tests/peering.test.js` predate the AU fork; don't attribute every passing behavior in these files to the fork.
- The AU-specific incident deltas inspected are mainly geographic/theme substitutions and removal of region-specific global events. Tropical power/typhoon events are eligible only at named northern cities; Australian summer is included among local fiber-cut narrative causes. These source diffs do not add a new general recovery primitive. The inherited tests exercise several concrete recovery contracts: redundant router survival, spare/crew-constrained owned repair, repair not clearing an independent shared-domain failure, LACP/loss/PMTUD failure and clear, and leased-wave control-plane outage distinct from owned fiber.
- The map-data build inputs are committed and generated deterministically; `scripts/build-au-map.js --check` is documented as the consistency check. The committed provenance note describes 121 POIs from an ACCC 2011 spreadsheet and its limits, as well as Natural Earth map data. Treat these as AU fork inputs/provenance, not SOC source-pack candidates.

## Transfer to Series of Calls

The NBN mechanic is useful as a **scenario pattern**, not an identified missing SOC transport foundation. Current [SOC `42-transport.js`](../../src/game/42-transport.js) already separates desired, provisioning, effective, and observed states; delays link activation; enforces hard path capacity; supports an active `access` Mbps contract and rejects allocations exceeding contract quantity; bills uncontracted links independently; and avoids double billing an access-contract-backed link. [SOC AC-SOC-131](../../tests/transport.test.js) specifically exercises contract capacity, over-allocation rejection, settlement, and duplicate-billing prevention. So do not port the NBN formulas or claim access capacity is absent.

The smallest plausible additional proposal, only if a reviewed source/requirement exposes a gap, is a synthetic **service activation over contracted access** acceptance scenario: a distant service/tenant attach point cannot carry a configured workload until a named access port is ordered; port capacity is independently provisioned and limits throughput after it becomes effective; an upgrade preserves the previous effective capacity until ready; and per-activated-service charges reconcile separately from access-port rent. Use fictional provider/service names and SOC dispatch, with desired/provisioning/effective/observed assertions, refusal without access, over-capacity failure, migration/restore validation, and recovery after upgrade. This would be a workload-level integration case over SOC42 and commercial settlement, not an NBN implementation.

Data-center racks are a weaker fit: SOC's synthetic communications estate currently models sites, links, and service resources, not hosting inventory or data-center rental. If future requirements demand it, borrow only the constrained-inventory pattern (ordered capacity, readiness date, reservation on contract activation, overcommit refusal, release after service end) using an existing synthetic communications resource. Do not add generic hosting to satisfy this fork survey.

Significant source behavior remains out of scope or unrepresented by the candidate patterns: all 121 geographic POIs and their premises/FSA distribution; Australian state progression, geography, land-aware route surveys, actual cable landing chains, subsea route economics and named peer exchanges; NBN's particular wholesale AVC model; ISP broadband retail/traffic economics; rack leasing, colocation and dedicated-server fulfillment; country-specific progression/badges and the fork's localization/game mode choices. These are distinctive AU gameplay content, not claims about missing or equivalent SOC telephony behavior. The source also removed puzzle/weekly/Architect/scenario modes; no deleted AU behavior should be mistaken for a feature to adopt.

## Source test techniques

The AU tests use Node's built-in test runner, a local `jsdom` harness that assembles the page from source, seeded `Math.random`, and direct game globals (`ACT`, `S`, `R`, `dayTick`) so they can assert mechanics without a real browser. The strongest reusable techniques are: assert refusal leaves money and state unchanged; check state before/during/after lead-time advancement; test capacity at saturation and after upgrade; reconcile recurring and per-service ledger totals; mutate saves with invalid entities then migrate and validate; assert decommission cleanup; check both internal state and rendered operator feedback; test six dictionaries at 360px; and pair service success with a negative and a recovery path. For SOC adaptation, preserve the behavioral matrix but send commands through the shared dispatcher and assert persisted/worker outcomes where required by the repo.
