# Provider integration support ownership

This adapter derives resource ownership from two existing paid Provider offers. It adds no free owner record and changes neither the offer catalogue nor the existing Orchard MSP scope.

| Resource owner path | Required native offer and evidence | Commercial terms | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recorder | `managed-uc-cc-integration`, completed with a valid native UC/CC compatibility test, autonomous captured customer call and paid same-case quality review. The tested Cisco contact-centre product maps to the existing `disco-vendor` vendor role. | A$250 setup, A$110 per period, 6 work minutes, 0 engineers, 2 support reservations. | `phone`, `recorder`, `archive`, `engage`; recorder responsibility is supplier/vendor scoped. |
| Meeting room | `integrator-room`, completed by Twig Integrations with a real completed meeting after commissioning. | A$650 setup, A$100 per period, 8 work minutes, 1 engineer, 1 support reservation. | `room`; named integrator responsibility. |

The versioned `integrator-room` choice `{version:1,roomMode:'bridge'}` preserves the meeting profile and is available only when the customer already has a paid, effective Cisco meetings interoperability product and bridge licence. The default operation retains its legacy native-room behavior. The bridge choice changes no offer price, staff terms or lead time.

The ordinary `uc-cc-integration` and `direct-room-integration` delivery offers do not create either owner. The former is a direct integration with no supplier-managed support term; the latter proves a room edge and meeting path but does not name a support owner. `recording` provisions capture and archive with no support reservation. None of these may be treated as owner evidence by themselves.

`SOC_PROVIDER_INTEGRATION_SUPPORT.supportFor(state, customerId, resourceIds, {at, hooks})` is pure. The root should call it only with private, state-derived hooks. Its UC/CC branch validates the managed project's paid posting and exact offer terms, then the native UC/CC receipt and retained task proof. Its room branch validates the exact paid integrator operation, named Twig role, paid posting and a completed room session whose `joinRoomGeneration` matches the paid room project generation. The meeting platform dependency is retained separately; it can name the phone resource and must not be mistaken for the room generation. `validateOwner(state, owner, {at, hooks})` reconstructs the corresponding owner from retained native records; it does not trust an owner row submitted by a command.

Current eligibility is separate from historical proof. At the current state clock, the customer and owned resource must still be active and effective, and the room route must still appear in the native responsibility evaluator. Historical evaluation uses an `at` no later than the current clock, the original paid project and actual delivery evidence at that time; later resource outage does not rewrite the earlier owner interval. A service retirement ends current scope at its recorded `effectiveAt`.
