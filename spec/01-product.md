# Product, learning and campaign

Series of Calls is a synthetic configuration training simulator embedded in a communications-business campaign. Its audience includes new UC administrators, service-desk staff, implementation engineers, solution designers and operators learning dependencies across suppliers. It teaches investigation and recovery as well as initial configuration. No lesson needs a real tenant, carrier, PSTN number, customer record or live AI service.

The campaign loop is discover needs, qualify a bid, design an eligible service, order and provision it, prove end-to-end outcomes, operate incidents and renew or change the contract. The learner begins as a regional MSP able to purchase connectivity. Physical network ownership is an optional expansion. Existing customer contracts remain active when another customer is acquired, so changes consume project capacity and create continuing support obligations.

Three modes share one engine. Guided lessons disclose prerequisites, explain diagnostic codes and suggest investigations without silently repairing configuration. Assessed challenges hide the reference solution and score observed outcomes, constraints, cost and continuity. Sandbox allows unrestricted valid configurations and incident injection, but does not bypass dimensional units or invent interoperability. A sandbox success is not campaign evidence unless evaluated against the same contract predicates.

## Learning objectives

Learners must distinguish order acceptance from provisioning and service readiness; find the first failed dependency without treating every warning as a root cause; separate signalling success from directional media; distinguish people, agent entitlements, trunk sessions and physical throughput; follow a case across channels; identify missing recording or processing obligations after a resolved conversation; recover a cohort migration while preserving unaffected service; and explain both operating margin and information boundaries.

Every assessed objective needs a reference solution and a meaningful counterexample. The reference solution is one solution, not an exact configuration fingerprint that the assessor requires. Routing through another compatible carrier, choosing a permitted local processor, providing a different room interop path or declining an optional feature may satisfy a contract. Reduced promised functionality is valid only after an explicit contract amendment permitted by the scenario.

## Campaign structure

The SMB track introduces a shared business number, a small service queue, appointment reminders, a fictional business-messaging channel and customer records. A demand shift exposes staffing constraints while voice still consumes the same people's time. Completion requires useful resolution, retained context, contractual response targets and sustainable cost.

The enterprise track introduces two installed UC families, legacy endpoints, mixed room systems, reception, recording and cohort migration. Discovery produces an inventory with confidence and unresolved identities. Cutover moves a scoped cohort after preflight tests. Voice-only validation is insufficient: old-to-new and new-to-old journeys, calendar identities, recording and access must still work. Recovery may restore the prior cohort configuration or repair dependencies; it must not erase valid evidence or current conversations.

The government track models citizen-service channels, languages, accessibility, permitted administrators, support responsibility and information handling. Eligibility precedes price scoring. A faster transcription offer can be rejected because of its processing location even when storage remains local. Outcomes include correctly retained and accessible artefacts, response targets and useful human escalation.

The defence track concerns synthetic administrative and training collaboration. The isolation exercise checks local call control, identity, DNS, trusted time, certificate validity, licensing, management and recording/processing permissions. Local media alone does not establish disconnected operation. On reconnection, queued records, identity changes and service recovery must preserve sequence and allowed destinations.

Jurisdiction variants are orthogonal to customer scale and workload. AU, US, JP and EU represent separately versioned profiles; government and defence are not global certifications. Lessons explain the fictional contract explicitly and link dated research separately.

## Current foundation exercises

The preview implements one narrow fault-and-repair lesson for each track: no available people; stale recording identity plus missing room calendar; excluded transcript region; and missing local licensing support. They demonstrate the engine contracts and are labelled foundation exercises. They do not implement the full procurement, migration, adoption, multi-contract or renewal campaign described above.
